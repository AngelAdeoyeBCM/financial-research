# EM rates: Exiting 2y IBR–TIIE-F payer

[The author] exit the 2y IBR–TIIE-F payer trade that was opened on 6 May (see EM rates: Entering 2y IBR–TIIE-F spread payer), booking a loss of 38bp (USD191k). [The author is] exiting [the 2y IBR–TIIE-F payer trade] as April inflation data in Colombia failed to act as a catalyst for higher rates. Furthermore, news on the Council of State striking down the pension funds transfer decree was sparking a leg down in the IBR curve. Even though the proximity of Colombia’s first-round presidential election on 31 May should drive further premium in the IBR curve, [the author prefers] to exit now. To recap, [the author] entered [the 2y IBR–TIIE-F payer trade] following the recent and surprising monetary policy pause in Colombia. The IBR curve should retain [the IBR curve's] premium, signaling either a higher terminal rate or stronger inflation ahead. With [Colombia’s first-round presidential election on 31 May] less than three weeks away, the premium seems further warranted given the potential for ongoing political pressure on the central bank under a new administration.
