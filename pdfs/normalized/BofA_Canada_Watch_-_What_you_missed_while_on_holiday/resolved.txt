BoC “minutes” show uncertainty about next rate move The Bank of Canada’s (BoC) Summary of Deliberations for the December meeting was released on December 23. The main takeaway is that the BoC is unclear when and in which direction the next policy rate move will be given the high level of uncertainty. The Governing Council reiterated that “uncertainty remained elevated and that they would assess incoming data relative to the Bank’s outlook.” The Governing Council continued to maintain that under the current BoC outlook the policy rate is at about the right level to keep inflation close to target while supporting the economy. [The author expects] the BoC to hold the policy rate at 2.25% for several months, while [the BoC] waits for data to assess where the economy is going.
