# EM rates: Taking profit on MXN basis steepener

As the MXN basis steepner position has moved in [the author's] favor, [the author is] exiting [the MXN basis steepner position] and booking a profit of 7.5bp (USD75k). Recent issuance of external debt by Mexican local corporates and an uptick in hedging that capitalizes on USDMXN weakness have driven the longer-tenor basis sharply higher, injecting steepening pressure into the curve. At the same time, front-end funding in Mexico has stabilized, limiting short-term downside risk. Consequently, [the author is] locking in profits by closing out now. To recap, [the author] entered [the MXN basis steepner position] on 14 January to position for the expected persistence of downward bias in the basis, supported by the typical seasonal dip in remittance inflows. [The author] also recognized that potentially sizable local external debt issuance in January could reduce downside pressure on the longer-end basis (see Mexico: Entering an MXN basis steepener trade).
