# FX: Taking profit on long AUDCAD

. 16/04/2026 1

RKE. [The author takes] 2.12% (USD212k) profit on [the author's] long AUDCAD trade idea [the author] opened on 19 March (see FX: CAD support to wane). The energy price shock from the Middle East-conflict has led the CAD to outperform the G10 complex, partly due to improved relative terms of trade. [The energy price shock from the Middle East-conflict] had benefited Canada more than other energy exporters, which [the author] thought was unjustified. Hence, [the author] saw scope for [the CAD] to come under pressure. [The scope for the currency to come under pressure] proved correct. Since the start of April, markets have been trading headlines suggesting potential de-escalation more actively. [That markets have been trading headlines suggesting potential de-escalation more actively] has supported [the author's] position as markets re-engage with carry trades, of which AUD offers the highest vol-adjusted yield in G10, and given that AUD has one of the highest sensitivities to global risk sentiment. [The author] would also point to CAD acting as a USD proxy as a reason for the price action. Given the scope of the rally and that [the author's] risk premium metrics have now moved to stretched levels, [the author is] now wary of some consolidation. As such, now is an opportune time to lock in profits. [The author still sees] scope for [AUDCAD] to extend higher in the medium term, as superannuation funds hedging away USD risk supports AUD structurally and because the CAD is particularly vulnerable to US trade policy. Ishan Gurnani, G10 FX Strategist | BNP Paribas Securities Corp
