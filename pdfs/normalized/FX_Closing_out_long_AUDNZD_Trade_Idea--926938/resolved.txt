# FX: Closing out long AUDNZD

. 24/06/2026 1

BNP P ARIBAS GLOBAL M ARKE TS AP P. [The author] close the trade idea [the author] initiated in FX: Adding a tactical AUDNZD long on 15 June for a profit of 0.72% (USD36k, spot ref: 1.22 22). The initial rationale for [the tactical AUDNZD long] was to position ahead of the Reserve Bank of Australia meeting and Q1 GDP report in New Zealand. The two events posed risks for front-end rates in opposite directions, which would have put upward pressure on [AUDNZD]. The outlook at the RBA meeting was more balanced and Q1 GDP in New Zealand came in stronger than anticipated. [The author finds] it encouraging that despite neither catalyst materialising in a direction that would be supportive, [AUDNZD] still trended higher, highlighting that [the author's] position was the correct one to capture the event asymmetry. [The author] held [the tactical AUDNZD long] through Australia’s May CPI report for a similar rationale but now opt to move to the sidelines ahead of the country’s May employment report. [The May employment report] poses more two-sided risks for spot than others. A stronger report could see front-end meeting pricing shift to reflect a greater probability of an additional hike – [the author remains] paid August RBA. However, if [the May employment report] were to surprise to the downside (for the second consecutive time), then there is scope for AUD longs to further unwind. [The author thus thinks] it makes sense to lock in [the author's] profits now. Ishan Gurnani, G10 FX Strategist | BNP Paribas Securities Corp.
