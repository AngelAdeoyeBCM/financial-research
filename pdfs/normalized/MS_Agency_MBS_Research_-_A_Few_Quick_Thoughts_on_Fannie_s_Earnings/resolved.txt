# Agency MBS Research - A Few Quick Thoughts on Fannie's Earnings

Agency MBS Research | North America

Fannie Mae reported 4Q25 earnings this morning. While the monthly volume summary showed a $30bn increase in securities, the 10k showed what is likely a $22bn increase in TBA shorts, implying that net basis risk increased by "only" $8bn.
