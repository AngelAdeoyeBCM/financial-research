In FX: CAD playbook for Q4 (dated 23 September), [the author] argued that front-end rate differentials were the largest driver of the CAD, and that there would be important implications for the currency now that Bank of Canada meetings are considered ‘live’ events. [The author thinks] that some combination of three conditions must be met in order for the BoC to deliver a hike at [the BoC's] 28 October meeting: evidence of second round effects on inflation, a rise in inflation expectations, and the outright level of energy prices remaining elevated. Into the risk events next week (Canada September CPI and the Q3 Business Outlook Survey) [the author thinks] there is a favorable asymmetry to be long CAD. A hotter CPI print with evidence of emerging second-round impacts or a rise in inflation expectations could drive the market to price [the BoC's 28 October meeting] as at least a toss-up, in [the author's] view. Even if neither of [evidence of emerging second-round impacts or a rise in inflation expectations] materialize, [the author doubts] markets would shift to fully pricing in a hold for October given Brent crude is still trading above USD105/bbl, over 10% higher than at the last meeting. The tone of the last meeting minutes suggested that if oil prices remained near current levels (or rose), the prolonged nature of the shock made it progressively more likely that such elevated energy prices would be passed through and increase upside risks to inflation, even if [that such elevated energy prices would be passed through and increase upside risks to inflation] was not yet fully visible in the data. Heading into [Canada September CPI and the Q3 Business Outlook Survey], [the author's] quantitative metrics also support a favorable entry for USDCAD downside. USDCAD has persistently traded rich versus [USDCAD's] STEERTM fair value, and according to [the author's] Global Positioning Tracker, CAD shorts are well held (−21, +/−50 scale). [The author thus thinks] it makes sense to scale into a tactical USDCAD short and deem it prudent to take profit on [the author's] GBPCAD call seagull (see FX: Long GBPCAD via 3m seagull, dated 27 August) for 13.5bp. [The author remains] long GBP via Long GBPUSD (dated 30 September) in spot.
