# FX: Closing out long INRIDR

. 04/03/2026 1

RKE. The long INRIDR 6m NDF trade idea [the author] opened in FX: Tariff and trade tailwinds for INR on 4 February has reached [the long INRIDR 6m NDF trade idea's] stop–loss and [the author] close [the long INRIDR 6m NDF trade idea] for a loss of 1.56%. [The author] initiated [the long INRIDR 6m NDF trade idea] to express [the author's] medium-term bullish view on the INR. [The author] felt that India’s trade headwinds were abating, and could become tailwinds for the economy over the medium term, with trade deals being struck with the US, EU and other countries. Economic data had also shown signs of improvement. Given a resilient global backdrop, that potentially favoured carry strategies, [the author] saw scope for portfolio inflows into INR. [The author] also thought seasonal repatriation by exporters ahead of the tax-year end in March could provide additional support to [the INR]. However, in the wake of conflict in the Middle East, oil prices have risen sharply, as has volatility [risen sharply], prompting investors to broadly de-risk. As India is a large oil importer – and with the BNP Paribas Global FX Positioning Tracker showing investors holding long INR and short IDR exposure into last weekend’s escalation – [INRIDR] has declined. [The author has] now reached the stop –loss level on [the author's] trade idea, so [the author] close out [the long INRIDR 6m NDF trade idea] at 181.719 (USDINR 6Aug26 NDF: 93.265, USDIDR 6Aug26 NDF: 16948, 20:44 GMT+8) for a loss of 1.56% (USD 156k). Should the risk environment stabilise, and oil prices moderate, [the author] would be inclined to re-add bullish INR exposure to [the author's] model portfolio.
