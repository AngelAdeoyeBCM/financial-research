# Sector Flows Flash Update: Markets internals shift back to "Tightening financial conditions" fears as "Goldilocks" hopes fade

Global Quantitative Macro Strategy

## CITI'S TAKE

[The author] give a flash update based on Citi equity sector flows. Both long only and hedge fund managers were net buyers last week. Long-only increased exposure in most sectors, especially Tech and Financials. Hedge funds bought back some Tech and added Industrials. Top three sectors based on the last 2m of flows were Health Care, Real Estate, and Materials, while Consumer Staples, Financials, and Tech were the bottom three. [The author's] market-implied regime analysis suggests that market internals are currently most correlated with the "Tightening financial conditions" regimes as earlier hopes of "Goldilocks"/"Recovery" have materially faded. Continued geopolitical risks have provided a tailwind to Energy, while a hawkish speech from Chair Warsh at Jackson Hole and a strong US employment report prompted markets to reprice rates higher, causing headwind to rates sensitive sectors like Utilities, Real Estate, and Tech. Taken together, the resulting sector rotation strengthened expectations of "Tighter financial conditions". [The author's] model signals based on recent flows now favor Health Care, Real Estate, and Materials vs. Consumer Staples, Financials, and Tech.
