# EM rates: Take profit on EURPLN xccy receiver

Lior Kohanan, Global Macro Strategist | MAR | Burak Baskurt CFA, Global Head of EM Strategy | MAR. [The author takes] profit on [the author's] EURPLN xccy basis receiver at 12bp for a gain of 21bp (USD206k). [The author] initiated the trade idea in EM rates: Receive EURPLN 6x9 xccy basis on 22 October 2025. The goal was to take advantage of the attractive roll in the front end driven by a combination of excess domestic PLN liquidity and EUR bond issuance by Polish banks to meet regulatory requirements, which makes the front-end of the basis curve very steep. Structurally, [the author still likes] [the EURPLN xccy basis receiver], but deem it prudent to take profit at [12bp] and move to the sidelines for now. The 6x9 receiver is close to becoming spot-start and will turn more sensitive to moves in funding and FX volatility. [The author thinks] the market is currently long-positioned into PLN carry trades – [the author has] also closed [the author's] EURPLN put seagull (see EM FX and rates: Portfolio adjustments due to Middle East events, dated 2 March). If weakness in PLN FX persists, more FX conversions into PLN by the Polish finance ministry may make funding volatile in the short term and push front end basis higher. Trade idea: Close EURPLN 3m xccy basis (start date 24-Apr-26) at 12bp (GMT 10:29).
