# FX: Adding to long USDJPY exposure on the dip

[The author thinks] risk-reward is attractive to buy the dip in USDJPY and therefore enter a call with an EKO. The case for USDJPY to appreciate remains strong, in [the author's] view, with the B ank of Japan still well behind the curve, JPY positioning neutral, on [the author's] metrics, and additional fiscal being deployed in Japan (see Japan: Persistent reflationism in an inflationary era, dated 23 July). To the extent that today’s USDJPY move reflects Ministry of Finance FX intervention which notably has not been confirmed, [the author argues] that without a change in monetary policy in Japan, USDJPY is unlikely to sustain losses for long, similar to what happened during April’s intervention. Additionally, [the author does] not expect recent news flow concerning Japan’s Government Pension Investment Fund to result in material FX strength (see Global Macro: The Great Reversal? Japan’s potential domestic pivot, dated 27 July). [The author sees] USDJPY continuing to grind higher and prefer to position for topside risks in USDJPY spot whilst staying short gamma. Front-end USDJPY risk reversals have moved further in favour of puts and screen rich (too bid-for-puts) compared to spot-vol correlation. [The author thinks] [that Front-end USDJPY risk reversals have moved further in favour of puts and screen rich (too bid-for-puts) compared to spot-vol correlation] indicates market concerns over potential FX intervention and GPIF asset reallocation, which could limit near-term topside in [USDJPY]. [The author thinks] markets are already expecting a hawkish hold at the July BoJ meeting, and for the JPY to benefit from a hawkish message [the BoJ] would need not only to discuss increasing the pace of rate hikes but more explicitly hint at the September meeting as being in play for a rate hike, which [the author] deem unlikely. In addition to [the author's] long USDJPY spot trade idea entered on 23 June at 161.36 level (see Adding long USD carry via long USDJPY and USDCHF), [the author now adds] a 3m (30 Oct) USDJPY 160 call with European KO at 163.50 in USD30mn for 24bp (spot ref: 159.42).
