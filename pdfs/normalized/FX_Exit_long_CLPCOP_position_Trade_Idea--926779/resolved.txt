# FX: Exit long CLPCOP position

[The author] exit [the author's] long CLPCOP position as [the author's long CLPCOP position] has breached the stop-loss level, booking a loss of 3.8% (USD379k). The CLPCOP has dropped sharply in recent sessions, partly due to the upside correction in USDCLP after a more hawkish-than-expected June FOMC meeting. On the COP side, after Colombia’s presidential run-off confirmed market expectations that the opposition candidate – and favourite – Abelardo de la Espriella (ADE) was likely to win, the COP initially experienced further outperformance. After the COP’s sharp rally in past weeks, [the author sees] room for a consolidation, now that the market’s expectations have been confirmed. [The author sees] possible some short-term reversal of the outperformance against key peers (CLP, BRL, MXN). To recap, [the author] entered [the author's long CLPCOP position] on 17 June, expecting the CLP to rebound after [the CLP's] notable underperformance since March, aided by lower oil prices, a strong copper price, and a favorable external backdrop. Meanwhile, stretched technicals in the COP and heavy positioning (reflecting a positive outlook for the Colombian election) had left [the COP] vulnerable to a technical rebound (see FX: Entering a tactical long CLPCOP position, dated 17 June).
