To summarise:
- Tariffs imposed on EU and UK exports to the US result in a negative demand shock for the euro area and the UK based on [the author's] analysis, with a negative effect on economic activity and inflation. While the effect via trade tends to be small when measured using standard economic models, in practice the size is hard to pin down as [the effect via trade] can be non-linear and increase with the size of tariffs, for example.
- If the EU and the UK were to retaliate imposing tariffs on imports from the US, [the EU and the UK's] external balance might improve, but the increase in import prices might weaken domestic consumption in the euro area and UK. Hence, the final impact on inflation in the euro area and the UK would be less clear.
