# FX: Exit long USDCOP position

[The author] exit the long USDCOP position that was opened on 4 May, booking a loss of 0.6% (USD17k). [The author is] exiting [the long USDCOP position] because [the long USDCOP position] has breached [the author's] stop-loss level. The sharp rally in COP over the past several days has renewed [COP's] outperformance versus peers. Given the first-round presidential election on 31 May is still some time away, [the author expects] the market has room to price in a higher risk premium and narrow [the outperformance versus peers], given the proximity of the COP to [the first-round presidential election on 31 May]. To recap, [the author] entered [the long USDCOP position] in anticipation of the COP trailing as Election Day nears (see FX: Going long BRLCOP, dated 4 May).
