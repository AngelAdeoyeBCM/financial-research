NOKSEK has been a key beneficiary of the market positioning for the energy trade in FX (long energy exporters versus short energy importers) since the outbreak of conflict in the Middle East. However [NOKSEK] has now rallied to around parity, a level [NOKSEK] has not persistently traded above since 2023. [NOKSEK] also trades expensive to [NOKSEK's] BNP Paribas STEER fair value of 0.9752 (Figure 1). Rich valuations and stretched long positioning in [NOKSEK] (Figure 2) suggest market positioning for the energy trade is now well reflected in [NOKSEK], [the author thinks]. At [around parity] [the author thinks] there is an asymmetry to the downside where if oil prices go up, given positioning and valuations [NOKSEK] may not rise much further whereas [NOKSEK] could be particularly vulnerable if oil prices fall. [NOKSEK] rallied after Norges Bank surprised the market by hiking [Norges Bank's] policy rate by 25bp to 4.25% at [Norges Bank's] May meeting. Norges Bank hawkishness is now well discounted in NOK in [the author's] view and the rally following [the May meeting] presents an attractive entry point to go short NOKSEK.
