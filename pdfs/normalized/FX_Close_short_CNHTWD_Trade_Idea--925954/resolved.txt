# FX: Close short CNHTWD

. 09/06/2026 1

RKE. [The author] close the trade idea [the author] initiated in FX: Tactical short CNHTWD on 20 April for a modest loss of 0.24% (USD24.3k out of USD10mn notional). [The author] opened [the Tactical short CNHTWD] expecting the TWD to outperform, driven by foreign equity inflows, Taiwanese policymakers’ efforts to curb inflation and favourable seasonality. [The author] used the CNH as a funder, as [the author] anticipated the People’s Bank of China will smooth the fall in USDRMB spot and the rise in the RMB index. [The Tactical short CNHTWD] performed in line with [the author's] expectations initially. However, stronger USD boosted the CNH, as Chinese exporters rushed to settle on rebound, a risk [the author] had highlighted in [FX: Tactical short CNHTWD]. For the TWD, several headwinds emerged: tech giants missed earnings expectations, concerns grew about an earlier peak in the chip super‐cycle and expectations of Fed tightening rose. [The several headwinds] sparked a correction in tech equities, triggering outflows from TWSE stocks and adding pressure on [the TWD]. [That the several headwinds sparked a correction in tech equities, triggering outflows from TWSE stocks and adding pressure on the TWD] has made the TWD an average performer compared to [the TWD's] Asian peers. [The author sees] further risks as investors rotate to higher-USD trades (see FX: The USD and rate hikes, dated 5 June). [The author still believes] that the CNH will outperform [the CNH's] Asian peers in a higher-USD environment, anchored by exporters’ settlement demand and prevailing RMB appreciation expectations. [The author thinks] that the PBoC is comfortable with the current appreciation pace, with the fixing trending downward. On the other hand, [the author sees] a modest near‐term upside for USDTWD amid a global USD recovery and Taiwanese authorities’ measures to contain two-way volatility.
