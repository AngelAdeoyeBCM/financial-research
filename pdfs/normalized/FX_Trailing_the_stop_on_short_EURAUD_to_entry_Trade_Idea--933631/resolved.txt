# FX: Trailing the stop on short EURAUD to entry

[The author] initiated a short EURAUD trade idea on 2 October to add downside EUR protection amid OAT-spread widening. Although the OAT-Bund spread has not re-widened since initiating [the short EURAUD trade idea], EUR crosses including EURAUD have remained heavy. From a positioning perspective [the author's] tracker suggests EUR shorts are historically stretched (see BNPP Global FX Positioning Tracker, dated 12 October), and EUR put convexity has built to a five-year high. However, [the author's] options strike map does not suggest that EURUSD strikes sub-1.10 are well owned (see FX vol strategy weekly: EUR downside dichotomy, dated 12 October) so [the author does] not yet have confidence that France risks are adequately hedged in EUR. [The author continues] to favour pairing EUR short exposure with longs in AUD as [the author is] tactically constructive on the AUD outlook while global equities stay supported, rate differentials are wide (leading to attractive carry on the crosses), and Australia benefits from reduced fiscal vulnerabilities compared to elsewhere. [The author has] increased [the author's] exposure to [pairing EUR short exposure with longs in AUD] through a EURUSD lower [or] AUDUSD higher dual digital (see FX vol roadmap: Fiscal spillovers, dated 13 October). [The author continues] to hold [the short EURAUD trade idea], targeting a move to 1.56, but deem it prudent to trail the stop to entry (1.61470), 1.19% from current spot (spot ref: 1.59678; 11:47 BST).
