# FX: Exit long USDPEN

[The author] exit [the author's] long USDPEN 1m NDF position, booking a loss of 3.1% (USD156k). The market’s assessment that Keiko Fujimori would win the runoff—based largely on the offshore vote count that skewed in [Keiko Fujimori's] favor—prompted a rapid slide in the USDPEN. The risk premium that had accrued after the initial count has now been stripped away, even though the final outcome is still pending. In fact, with election-related bearish pressure now gone, [the author's] STEER fair-value model flags the PEN as the most overvalued currency in the region. To recap, [the author] initiated [the long USDPEN 1m NDF position] on 5 June because [the author] believed that the low election-related risk premium priced in to local assets, coupled with already sizable net-long positions and potential profit-taking after the runoff, had created a negatively asymmetric risk profile in the wake of the election.
