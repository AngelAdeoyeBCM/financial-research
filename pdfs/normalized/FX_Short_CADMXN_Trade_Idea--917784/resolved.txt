## KEY MESSAGES

- [The author sees] a favorable asymmetry of risk for the MXN relative to the CAD, as trade negotiations with the US are likely to weigh on Canada more than on Mexico.
- Strong US data create a supportive backdrop for carry, from which the MXN can benefit in the Latam complex.
- Trade idea: Short CADMXN Entry 12.7266; target 12 stop-loss 13.15; total allocation USD5mn. Top up: 5mn at 12.95. [The author] initiate a short CADMXN trade to capture [the author's] view of a favor able asymmetry of risk for the MXN relative to the CAD as negotiations on the US–Mexico–Canada trade agreement advance, coupled with further room for carry trades to perform. The pullback in [CADMXN] following last week’s price action creates a favorable entry level, in [the author's] view. Even though the US Trade Representative’s statement suggests that USMCA renewal is in the best interest of all parties, [the author sees] scope for market volatility in the coming months due to uncertainty and the possibility that the US negotiating position might include suggesting breaking up [the US–Mexico–Canada trade agreement] or bilateral deals. [The coming months of market volatility due to uncertainty] would likely weigh on Canada more than Mexico, given the apparent difference in political favor from the US administration. For instance, [the author notes] two contrasting Truth Social posts from President Donald Trump last week, one discussing a “productive telephone conversation” with President Claudia Sheinbaum that went “extremely well”, and the other raising the prospect of 50% US import tariffs on Canadian aircraft. [The apparent difference in political favor from the US administration] is consistent with what the administration has said thus far and what [the author thinks] may remain a key difference between the US trading partners during the ongoing USMCA negotiations. Despite last Friday’s wash-out of long metals and short USD positions following the confirmation of Kevin Wash as Fed chair, [the author expects] the continuation of a range-bound USD against G10, which would support carry positions (CADMXN offers 5% on an annualized basis). Likewise, [the author expects] the US cyclical position to remain strong, supporting the MXN through the trade and remittances channels. Finally, [the author notes] that in the next months the MXN could take the lead as a Latam carry proxy due to volatility related to the political cycles in Brazil and Colombia.
