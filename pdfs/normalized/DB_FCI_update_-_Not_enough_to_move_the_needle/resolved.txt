# FCI update: Not enough to move the needle

Economics US Economic Notes Date 6 February 2026

- In this note, [the author] update [the author's] high-frequency version of the Fed's FCI-G to quantify the impact on expected growth from recent moves in asset prices. Over the last week, equities have sold off, the dollar has unwound some of [the dollar's] January depreciation, and credit spreads have widened.
- While [the recent moves in asset prices] have tightened financial conditions, [the recent moves in asset prices] have had a relatively minor impact on the Fed's measure, with both the baseline and alternative index tightening by only 3bps over the past week.
- Even with [the tightening in financial conditions over the last week], financial conditions remain easy and would be expected to add up to a percentage point to the growth outlook over the next year. As such, a more pronounced move in markets is likely need ed to meaningfully impact the Fed outlook. FCI update In this note, [the author uses] [the author's] high frequency version of the Fed's FCI-G to provide an update on recent moves in financial conditions and quantify [the recent moves in financial conditions'] impact on expected growth. As a reminder, the Fed's financial conditions index (FCI-G) is intended to translate contemporaneous and lagged changes in financial variables into [the contemporaneous and lagged changes in financial variables'] impact on growth. For more details about the construction of [the author's] daily version, see "What a difference a week makes for the Fed's FCI". Financial conditions this easy likely make Fed officials slightly less concerned about a sharp deterioration on the employment side of [Fed officials'] mandate, especially when paired with recent preliminary signs of stabilization in the labor market data. The tightening in financial conditions over the last week would either need to be sustained for a meaningful length of time or show signs of impacting market functioning for the Fed to begin signalling [the Fed's] concern about financial conditions.
