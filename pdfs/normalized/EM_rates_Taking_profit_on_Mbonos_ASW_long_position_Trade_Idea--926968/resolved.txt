# EM rates: Taking profit on Mbonos ASW long position

[The author takes] profit on [the author's] 2034 Mbonos-10y TIIE-F asset swap receiver position after [the asset-swap spread] moved in [the author's] favour, booking a gain of 6.4bp (USD96k). The asset-swap spread tightened as the bond curve outperformed the swap curve while broader rates rallied. [The author still believes] that, with Banxico having completed [Banxico's] easing cycle, Mbonos have the potential to outperform the swap curve, although a weakening MXN could decrease that outperformance marginally. [The author] chose to lock in profits now, ahead of the Banxico meeting and the potential upcoming bond-switch operation. A less hawkish-than-expected Banxico tone or a bond-switch program that sells long-end bonds could weigh on the asset-swap spread. [The author] will wait for a more attractive entry point. To recap, [the author] entered [the author's 2034 Mbonos-10y TIIE-F asset swap receiver position] on 1 June, noting that spread tightening remained the main risk amid US-Iran talks. [The author also expects] Mbonos to bounce back after [Mbonos'] recent lag, following the usual pattern after the government’s bond-swap operation. The 4 June coupon payment should bolster the Mbono curve as investors redeploy the proceeds (see EM rates: Entering long 2034 Mbonos ASW trade).
