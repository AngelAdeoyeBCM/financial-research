# FX: Trail stop on short AUDZAR to entry

[The author] initiated a short AUDZAR trade idea on 7 May to reflect [the author's] view that positioning and valuations had become stretched after [AUDZAR] benefited from higher energy prices. [The author's] rationale was that fresh gains would be capped, in the event of rising energy prices, but that declines in [AUDZAR] may be considerable if prices fell. Since then, energy prices have receded and [AUDZAR] has indeed fallen. [The author still thinks] [the asymmetry in the cross] is in play, and continue to hold [the short AUDZAR trade idea]. [The author's] metrics suggest positioning is still long (see Figure 1), but [the author] deem it prudent to trail the stop to the entry level of 11.8342. [The author continues] to target a move sub- 11.50, to 11.30.
