. 10/07/2026 1

RKE. Technicals had prompted [the long PLNZAR trade idea], with [the author's] positioning and valuation indicators showing stretched levels (see FX: Long PLNZAR on stretched technicals, dated 1 July). [The author] also felt divergence between the Polish and South African central banks was well priced in. However, Glapinski has now clearly opened the door for a September rate cut and it was notable that [NBP governor Adam Glapiński] chose not to talk down the possible negative impact on [the zloty]. [The author] therefore no longer think 4.30 will remain a sticky level for EURPLN and believe the zloty may continue to underperform.
