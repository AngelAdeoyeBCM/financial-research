# EM rates: Closing SIGB 10s30s flattener

Sources: Bloomberg, Macrobond, BNP Paribas Fig. 1: 10s30s seasonality in SIGB curve did not play out in 2025 Chandresh Jain, EM Asia FX and Rates Strategist | MAR | Joey Li, Markets Analyst, APAC | BNP Paribas Singapore Branch. [The author] close [the author's] SIGB 10s30s flattener trade idea (see EM rates: Enter a 10s30s SIGB flattener, dated 26 September) for a 16.5bp loss, as [the SIGB 10s30s flattener trade idea] has reached [the author's] stop. [The author] initiated [the SIGB 10s30s flattener trade idea] after the Monetary Authority of Singapore’s 50y SIGB auction in September. Seasonality analysis suggested that the long-end part of the SIGB curve could flatten, as MAS usually doesn’t auction super-long bonds in Q4 and Q1. While [the SIGB curve] flattened somewhat heading into [the Monetary Authority of Singapore’s 50y SIGB auction in September] and in the days after, the broader flattening move in 10s30s did not play out as [the author] had expected (see Figure 1). As such, [the author] close [the author's] position (long 30y SIGB 3.25% June 2054 and short 10y SIGB 2.75% March 2035) at 27.5bp for a 16.5bp loss (USD330k).
