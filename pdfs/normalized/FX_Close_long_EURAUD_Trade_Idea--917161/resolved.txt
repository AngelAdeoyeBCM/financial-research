# FX: Close long EURAUD

[The short AUD trade] was to capture [the author's] view that Australia superannuation funds would be an ongoing source of AUD selling flows (amid stable hedge ratios) and that positioning and valuations were stretched. [The report from the second-largest Australian superannuation fund] has likely contributed to recent AUD outperformance and leads [the author] to move to the sidelines in AUD.
