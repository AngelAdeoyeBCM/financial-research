# FX: Closing BRL leg of long BRLCOP position

As the BRL is reacting negatively to the latest headlines potentially impacting Flavio Bolsonaro’s candidacy to become president of Brazil, [the author is] closing the short USDBRL leg of [the author's] long BRLCOP position. [The author] book a loss of 0.4% (USD12k). [The author] exit as increased momentum seems possible, potentially impacting Bolsonaro’s chances. [The author sees] the BRL as susceptible to a correction given that [the BRL's] technical position is overbought. [The author is] keeping the long USDCOP leg as [the author expects] the COP to continue correcting the sharp outperformance of the past months due to the proximity of Colombia’s presidential election (31 May). Given heightened uncertainty about the outcome and the bi-modal impact for the market, [the author expects] [the COP] to underperform further, reflecting concern around [Colombia’s presidential election]. [The author] entered [the long BRLCOP position] on 4 May, expecting the BRL to continue performing well amid a positive terms of trade shock due to high oil prices and the currency’s high carry. On the COP side, [the author's] expectation was for underperformance as Election Day nears (see FX: Going long BRLCOP, dated 4 May).
