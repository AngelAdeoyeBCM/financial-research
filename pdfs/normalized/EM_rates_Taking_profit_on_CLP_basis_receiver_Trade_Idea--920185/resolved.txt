# EM rates: Taking profit on CLP basis receiver

[The author is] taking profit on [the author's] CLP basis receiver trade, which has moved in [the author's] favor on the back of weakness in the currency, locking in a profit of 8bp (USD40k). Following developments in the Middle East and the ensuing broader EM FX sell-off, the CLP basis has moved sharply to the downside. [The author still expects] further downside pressure on the CLP, as Chilean pension funds (AFPs) are likely to increase FX hedging to narrow the gap with [Chilean pension funds (AFPs)'s] foreign assets. However, [the author is] exiting [the CLP basis receiver trade] now to book a profit before any potential reversal of USDCLP price action. [The author] will continue to monitor the market for a suitable opportunity to re-enter [the CLP basis receiver trade]. To recap, [the author] entered [the CLP basis receiver trade] on 19 February anticipating increased FX hedging by AFPs, which is putting downward pressure on the CLP basis (see EM rates: Entering a CLP basis receiver).
