[The author believes] market positioning is paid, as markets are worried about back-to-back rate hikes from the BoK. As some of [the external factors that were driving KRW rates higher] soften, the BoK may not signal back-to-back hikes today, which could trigger a rally in KRW rates. Although [the author maintains] [the author's] view that the fundamental drivers of strong growth in South Korea remain intact, the market is still pricing 5 rate hikes from the BoK, which [the author thinks] is excessive versus [the author's] base case of 2 rate hikes.
