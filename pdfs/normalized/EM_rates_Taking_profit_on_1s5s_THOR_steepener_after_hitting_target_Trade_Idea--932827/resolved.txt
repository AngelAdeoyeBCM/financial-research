# EM rates: Taking profit on 1s5s THOR steepener after hitting target

[The author] close [the author's] 24-Jun-26, 1s5s Thai Overnight Repurchase Rate (THOR) steepener trade idea at 75bp for a profit of 22bp (USD440k), as [the 24-Jun-26, 1s5s THOR steepener trade idea] has reached [the author's] target (see EM rates: Green shoots in Thailand; pay 1s5s, dated 24 June). [The author] initiated [the 24-Jun-26, 1s5s THOR steepener trade idea] on [the author's] expectation that Thai economic growth would be supported by a surge in FDI into high-value-added sectors such as data centres, cloud services and electric vehicles. [The author] anticipated steepening pressure on the curve, driven by the widespread view that the Bank of Thailand would remain on hold amid weak domestic consumption, coupled with a deteriorating fiscal outlook as the debt-to-GDP ratio trends towards the 70% ceiling. [The 24-Jun-26, 1s5s THOR steepener trade idea] has performed in line with [the author's] expectations. Credit growth in Thailand has continued to accelerate, driven by lending to businesses (Figure 1). Additionally, Q2 GDP growth exceeded market consensus, which was acknowledged by the BoT during the most recent MPC meeting. And despite rising inflation, the BoT has indeed maintained a neutral stance on policy rates. Globally, elevated oil prices and a hawkish Federal Reserve have also put upward pressure on yields, with global as well as domestic long-end yields rising substantially. Consequently, [the author's] 24-Jun-26, 1s5s THOR steepener trade idea has reached [the 24-Jun-26, 1s5s THOR steepener trade idea's] target (Figure 2). [The author maintains] a steepener bias, but think it is prudent to take profit at this stage and re-enter at more attractive levels. [The author] close [the 24-Jun-26, 1s5s THOR steepener trade idea] at 75bp for a profit of 22bp (USD440k), including carry and rolldown.
