# FX: Close out short USDKRW

. 12/01/2026 1

RKE. [The author] close [the author's] short USDKRW (26May25 NDF) idea for flat P&L, with [USDKRW] having reached [the author's] trailed-to-entry stop level. On 21 November 2025, [the author] initiated a short USDKRW 6m NDF trade idea, with a view that the flow picture would improve over the medium term, allowing the KRW to better reflect [the KRW's] underlying fundamentals – see FX: Fading KRW weakness, sell USDKRW 6m NDF for the full rationale. Since then, local authorities’ growing concern over the imbalanced flow picture and currency weakness has resulted in a number of measures being announced to encourage repatriation and investment in local assets. Reports also suggest the NPS has started strategic hedging and that the BoK intervened in the FX market to defend the won. [The measures being announced to encourage repatriation and investment in local assets] prompted a sharp decline in USDKRW from 1480 to 1435. After [the sharp decline in USDKRW from 1480 to 1435], coming into the new year, [the author] viewed it prudent to trail the stop on [the author's] trade to [the author's] entry level (1460.50) – see FX: Trail stop on short USDKRW (dated 7 January). [USDKRW] has seen a sharp retracement over the last few sessions, and [the author has] now reached [the author's] trailed-stop level. [The author thinks] [the sharp retracement over the last few sessions] reflects local retail investors’ vociferous appetite for foreign assets, and possible position washout of KRW longs. Indeed, BNP Paribas Global FX Positioning Analysis suggests FX investors have reduced KRW longs from a score of +15 to +6 (on a +/ -50 scale) since late December. [The author] respect [the author's] stop and close [the author's] trade idea for a flat P&L. However, [the author continues] to believe the flow picture will improve over the coming months, as [the measures being announced to encourage repatriation and investment in local assets] get implemented, and later due to WGBI inflows and the risk of the NPS pivoting [the NPS's] asset allocation in favour of domestic assets. The pace of [the sharp retracement over the last few sessions] might also make authorities uncomfortable, and raise the risk of further intervention to stabilise the currency. In turn, [the author] will be keen to re-engage in KRW longs at more stretched valuations.
