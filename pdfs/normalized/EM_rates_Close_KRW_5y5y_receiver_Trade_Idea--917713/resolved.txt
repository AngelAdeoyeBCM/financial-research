[The author] initiated [the received position in KRW H31,5y NDIRS] in January, following a hawkish Bank of Korea policy meeting, as [the author] believed that the market's pricing of more than two rate hikes by end-2027 was excessive (see EM rates: Receive KRW 5y5y as it may be capped, dated 15 January). [The author's] view remains that the current policy rate remains appropriate, and [the author sees] a high bar for [the Bank of Korea] to move rate in either direction during this period. [The author] also expected WGBI inflows to support a rally in the long-end. KRW rates have sold off, given [KRW rates'] correlation with the JGB curve and talks about a supplementary budget in Korea amid President Lee's recent comments. Although KRW rates experienced a relief rally after the JGBs calmed and USDKRW moved lower, the USD's subsequent strengthening amidst a broad-based risk-off environment and squaring of positions in consensus trades led to a USDKRW rebound and another sell-off in KRW rates (Figure 1). The upward move in oil prices also contributed to the sell-off in KRW rates from an inflation perspective. While [the author's] medium-term view on back-end KRW rates remains unchanged, recent market dynamics have highlighted the potential for other factors, such as global fiscal concerns and FX moves, to dominate price action in the near term. Therefore, [the author] close [the author's] H31,5y receiver trade idea at 3.73% and will look to re-enter at more favorable levels.
