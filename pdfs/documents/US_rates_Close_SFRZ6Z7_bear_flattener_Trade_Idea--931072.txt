# US rates: Close SFRZ6Z7 bear flattener

We close our SFRZ6Z7 bear flattener as the trade has reached our stop-loss level at -5bp. We entered the trade on 10 August (see US rates: Curve your enthusiasm) to position for a pulled forward hiking cycle, as we thought the market pricing was too backloaded at that moment, and if the Fed wanted to restore credibility, it require d it to act early and with more hikes. During the rather eventful August, we have an in-line July CPI, twist-turned July/Aug NFPs, a surprise buyback announcement, and a hawkish Jackson Hole speech. In a nutshell, t he numbers are still pointing towards a strong economy, which keeps the door open for the Fed to act early. However,

1. the market interpretation is that hikes are re-distributed across the whole of 2027, rather than pulled forward to 2026; and
2. the vol pick up at the strikes we entered, due to the vol differential across the two expiries, was depleted by the recent move. Trade: Close long SFRZ6 95.9375 Put vs Short 0QZ6 95.75 Put, equal-weighted. Entry: 2.5bp. Close -2.5bp. PnL: -5bp. Size: $75k/01.
