# FX: Trail stop and extend target on KRW longs

## TRADE IDEA

1/28/2026 1

Sources: Seibro, BNP Paribas Fig. 1: Retail outflows have moderated in recent weeks

## KEY MESSAGES

We deem it prudent to trail the stop on our KRW longs. The KRW has sharply appreciated, helped in part by recent concerns about potential for coordinated intervention by the US and Japan to support the JPY. We keep our medium-term bullish view on the KRW as we still see support from an improving flow picture, from policy-guided domestic capital reshoring and from the memory super-cycle. NPS adjustments to asset allocation and rebalancing should also help address market concerns around their USD demand. We favour extending the targets on our KRW longs versus the USD and CNH, to 1380 and 201, respectively. This week’s 25% tariff threat from the US poses a risk to our view, but Korean officials have signaled urgency to ratify the countries’ trade deal. We re-entered a long KRW position on 21 January (FX: Re-entering long KRW, funded with USD and CNH. The KRW has sharply appreciated since, in line with our medium-term bullish view on the currency. We see support from an improving flow picture, with policies to guide long-term capital back into Korean assets, and from the robust chip super-cycle. Also, in the past few sessions, reports of US ‘rate checks’ on USDJPY sparked market concerns that coordinated US–Japan intervention to support the yen may be imminent. This has read-across to the won in light of US Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent’s comment that the KRW valuation was not in line with fundamentals. With our position having performed, we deem it prudent to trail the stop to 1445 and 210 for USDKRW and CNHKRW, locking in minimum gains of 1.06% and 0.85%, respectively. We also extend the targets to 1380 and 201. NPS tailwinds: The National Pension Service (NPS) January committee delivered measures we see as favorable for the KRW, by deciding to:

- Raise the 2026 domestic equity weighting by 0.5pp, cut overseas equities by 1.7pp, and increase the domestic bonds allocation by 1.2pp.
- Temporarily suspend rebalancing when strategic asset allocation tolerance bands are reached
- Monitor market conditions in H1 and re-examine the SAA bands and related parameters. We think these measures remove concerns that KOSPI outperformance could trigger NPS sales of local equities to buy USD for rebalancing into overseas assets. The NPS said it had been guided by a comprehensive assessment of fund returns, the existing investment direction, and by the fund’s rapid growth and influence on the market. We note that these factors may also guide the medium-term asset allocation review due in May. Tariff threat: The market has largely shrugged off US threats of a fresh 25% tariff linked to perceived delays ratifying the countries’ trade deal, with Korean officials signaling legislation could pass in early February. However, we recognize the threat as a tail risk that would harm sentiment in KRW longs. Parisha Saimbi, EM Asia FXLM Strategist | MAR | BNP Paribas Singapore Branch | Franco Hsu, APAC Markets Strategist | Ann Jiang, APAC Markets Strategist | BNP Paribas Hong Kong Branch.
