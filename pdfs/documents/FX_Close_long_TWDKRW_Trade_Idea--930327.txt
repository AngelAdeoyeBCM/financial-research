# FX: Close long TWDKRW

We close the trade idea we opened in FX: Defying gravity, Long TWDKRW on 12 August for a loss of USD88k (0.88%). The KRW has outperformed its Asian peers, driven by robust USD selling from exporters after USDKRW breached 1,400 against our expectations. We see medium-term support for the KRW, as the scale of corporate shareholder return programs in the South Korean tech sector suggests potential FX appreciation. As the USDKRW has declined by 12%, exporter selling is becoming more opportunistic, in our view. We anticipate future FX flows will be governed by three primary drivers: equity market volatility, outbound retail investment, and exporter sentiment. Given the current downside risk skew for USDKRW – supported by USD selling amid a softening DXY – we close our long TWDKRW. Meanwhile, we stay positive on TWD, as its core drivers remain intact. Seasonal USD selling pressure from dividend repatriations has decelerated from the July peak; the final significant outflow (estimated at USD0.9bn) is likely to occur on 28 August. Further, cross-border equity flows into TWSE equities have turned supportive, bolstered by recent inflows following the August MSCI index review. Exporter USD selling has moderated after USDTWD breached 32, a move we attribute to uncertainty surrounding USDJPY. However, we maintain a downside skew outlook for USDTWD, as we anticipate recent US Treasury actions on the long end will cap USD-Asia topside. Also, the depreciation of USDKRW and USDCNH is likely to incentivise corporate FX conversions in Taiwan too. Finally, the CBC may hold a bias towards TWD strength to manage mounting inflationary pressures. We look to enter TWD longs again at better levels.
