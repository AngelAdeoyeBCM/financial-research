# Taking profit on long USDCHF, staying long USDJPY

On 23 June we added longs in both USDJPY and USDCHF spot, having previously traded USDJPY from the long side throughout the year (see Adding long USD carry via long USDJPY and USDCHF). At the time we saw room for long USD carry to perform over the summer. However, as argued in July FOMC: It’s like a free ride when you’ve already paid on 29 July, without a suggestion of imminent rate hikes from the FOMC, the USD may now come under some pressure given positioning which is currently long USD, according to our metrics (a score of +24/±50). As such, we deem it prudent to take profit on our USDCHF longs for a gain of 0.84% at 0.8135 (USD 84k). However, we remain long USDJPY with the BoJ still well behind the curve and given that option positioning above 164 appears light to us (Figure 2). We do not expect recent news flow around Japan’s Government Pension Investment Fund to result in material FX strength (see Japan: GPIF asset allocation scenarios, dated 17 July) and think the impact of any potential finance ministry intervention is likely to be short-lived unless accompanied by considerably lower US rates.
