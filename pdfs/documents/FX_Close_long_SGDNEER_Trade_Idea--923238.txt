# FX Close long SGDNEER Trade Idea

TRADE IDEA TRADE IDEA We are closing our long SGD position against a proxy version of the NEER basket for a loss of 0.85% (USD170k), including carry, as it has traded through our stop – see FX: Long SGDNEER ahead of April MAS meeting, dated 17 March. We initiated the trade in March, as we saw risks for the Monetary Authority of Singapore (MAS) to reduce the accommodation of its monetary policy at its April meeting, amid domestic reflation and rising oil prices due to the Middle East conflict. We believed that the market might be underestimating the risk of a re-centering move by the MAS, which would likely have led to significant appreciation in the SGD. The MAS did tighten policy in April – however, they only announced a modest increase in the SGDNEER's slope, which was on the more measured side of market expectations. The trade performed inversely to risk at first, underperforming when the Middle East conflict escalated and the USD rallied, due to the USD's weight in the basket (Figure 2). After the ceasefire news, however, the SGD underperformed other currencies like EUR and AUD, which rebounded significantly, despite the USD weakening, leading to underperformance of the basket again. Most recently, the sharp appreciation of the JPY, following intervention warnings has pushed the total return on our position through its stop. As such, we close the position (see Figure 1 for levels) for a 0.85% loss (USD170k), including carry.
