# FX: Take profit on long USDJPY

. 03/06/2026 1

RKE. We close the trade idea we opened in FX: Buying the dip in USDJPY on 30 April for a 2.38% gain (USD 238k) after the pair reached our 160 target. We initiated the position shortly after media reports suggested the Japanese Ministry of Finance had intervened in markets to strengthen the JPY. Our rationale was based on historical precedent that suggests fading unilateral intervention is a favorable strategy so long as it is not accompanied by a shift in the macroeconomic backdrop. Fundamentals have remained bullish for the pair. Energy prices remain elevated due to constrained flows through the Strait of Hormuz, weighing on Japan’s terms of trade. US data has continued to be strong and has driven markets to price in tighter monetary policy by the Federal Reserve. There has been an uptick in hawkish commentary by Bank of Japan officials, but we think this has been insufficient to support the JPY on its own, largely because the BoJ is still being viewed as behind the curve. Indeed, our own forecast for a 2% terminal rate (above consensus) to be reached after hikes every 4–5 months is likely insufficient to tackle inflationary pressures. In our view, USDJPY has further space to appreciate. However, the market may grow concerned about another intervention episod e at current levels of spot and thus we think it makes sense to lock in our profits here. We remain long USDJPY through our options portfolio via a 160 call with 163.50 EKO and a 163 digi call KO and are biased to re-engage with outright USDJPY longs if any dips were to present themselves.
