# EM rates: Stopping out of 3y INR NDS receiver

We close our receiver position in the INR z26, 2y NDS at 7.20%, realising a 29bp loss (USD290k) after hitting our stop-loss level. We initiated this trade earlier this month, expecting recent RBI measures to support a rally in the NDS curve (see India: RBI measures to push NDS rates lower, dated 3 July). While inflows into Indian government bonds (IGBs) have continued (Figure 1), market scepticism grew regarding the scale of FCNR inflows due to mixed reports and recent meetings between the RBI governor and various banks. Additionally, re-escalating tensions in the Middle East have pushed oil prices towards the USD80–90 range, exerting upward pressure on INR rates (Figure 2). However, we still believe that significant FCNR inflows will eventually materialise, as the RBI is incentivised to use these measures to curb currency depreciation. Furthermore, the RBI’s subsidised hedging facilities for external commercial borrowings (ECBs) should reduce hedging flows and support the NDS curve, in our view. That said, we respect our stop-loss level and will seek better opportunities to re-engage.
