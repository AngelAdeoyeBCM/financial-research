# EM rates: Close 1y1y TIIE-F receiver

As our 1y1y TIIE-F receiver trade has reached the stop loss level, we are exiting now, booking a loss of 28bp (USD140k). The Mexican curve has sold-off sharply in recent sessions as premium in EM curves increased with oil prices moving upward. While we still see Mexico’s weak cyclical backdrop and Banxico’s inclination to keep easing as support for a lower terminal rate (market: 6.85% vs. BNPP: 6.50%), we are closing the position now given lingering uncertainty over the Middle East backdrop. To recap, we entered the trade on 10 March. We think TIIE-F is a good expression for fading some of the premium accumulated in EM curves, given the Mexican economy’s weak cyclical position and Banxico’s willingness to cut rates further (see EM rates: Enter 1y1y TIIE-F receiver).
