# EM strategy: Close KRW m26 x z26 implied yields

Chandresh Jain, EM Asia FX and Rates Strategist | MAR | Joey Li, Markets Analyst, APAC | BNP Paribas Singapore Branch. We close our trade idea to receive KRW FX implied yields, as it has traded through our stop-loss level. We initiated the idea in December, when the spread between implied yields and the Bank of Korea’s policy rate widened to record highs (see EM strategy: Receive KRW m26 x z26 implied yields, dated 3 December 2025). At the time, we believed that the market’s pricing of rate hikes for 2026 seemed a lot and expected the WGBI inflows to be supportive of rates and lower implied yields. We had also expected the idea to serve as a risk-off hedge, which typically benefits from stronger USD demand, lower implied yields, and a wider cross-currency basis. Although the trade idea showed promise initially, it failed to perform in the current risk-off environment, driven by escalating tension in the Middle East. The surge in oil prices has increased inflation risks globally, leading to a sharp sell-off in rates across the world and a corresponding increase in implied yields. Markets are now pricing in close to five rate hikes from the BoK over the next 12 months – which continues to look extreme relative to fundamentals, in our view. However, given the current market dynamics, we think fading this move is not attractive, as price action is likely to remain driven by the ongoing conflict in the Middle East and oil prices, rather than fundamentals. Therefore, we respect our stop-loss level and close the idea at 2.55% for a loss of 21.8bp (-USD218k).
