# Close long AUDJPY for a small gain

We close our long AUDJPY spot position as the cross has reached our trailed-stop at entry (112.15). We exit the trade with a small carry gain of 0.23% (USD23k). This week USDJPY has dropped back towards the lows seen after the July intervention, contrary to our expectation for a re-weakening of the JPY. Market pricing for the Bank of Japan policy path has converged towards our view for a quarterly pace of rate hikes, in September, December and March (see BoJ to hike to 1.75% by spring 2027; terminal rate of 2.50% by Q3 2028, dated 6 August). We would expect such a pace of BoJ hikes to cap the potential peak in USDJPY, presenting a downside risk to our year-end USDJPY forecast of 165, but we note that the US remains an equally important driver of USDJPY. In this regard, rising US yields as the Fed embarks on a hiking cycle should keep USDJPY supported overall. Japan’s GPIF may seek to boost domestic allocations in the near term, but if the giant pension fund pursues this by using the upper limits of existing allocation ranges – rather than shifting its strategic asset allocation target midpoints – we doubt the scale of flow implie d would be meaningful for the currency over the medium-term (see Global Macro: The Great Reversal? Japan’s potential domestic pivot, 27 July).
