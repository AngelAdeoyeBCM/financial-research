# EM rates: Taking profit on HUF 5y receiver

We close our receive HUF 5y IRS trade idea for a profit of 9bp (or USD45k). We opened the position on 20 April, receiving M26 5y IRS (see EM rates: The case for HUF receivers), then rolled it on 17 June to receive the U26 5y IRS contract (see EM rates: Rolling M26 to U26 for HUF receivers). After the announcement of Hungary’s 2027 draft budget earlier this week, we see a lack of directional catalysts for this trade in the short term, and expect the NBH to keep rates on hold until the external backdrop becomes more favorable. We continue to like the fiscal consolidation story as the new government pursues eurozone convergence and we continue to express this view through our long 10y HGB vs bund (4 June) and short EURHUF via options (13 August) trade ideas.
