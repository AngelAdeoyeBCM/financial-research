# FX: Adjusting levels for our short CLPCOP position

We are trailing the stop on our short CLPCOP NDF position, adjusting the stop-loss to 3.417 (from 3.626) and setting a new take-profit target of 3.232 (versus 3.309). The pair has moved favorably to the downside, supported by a weakening USD and rising oil prices. This momentum is driven largely by persistent carry-seeking behavior, fueled by the significant negative interest-rate spread between Chile and Colombia. Additionally, diverging commodity trends have bolstered the position, as Colombia benefited from higher energy prices while Chile faced relative headwinds. Since inception, the trade has generated a gain of 5.8% (USD581k). Given that carry-seeking flows are expected to persist in the near term – in the absence of a significant USD rally – we are trailing our levels to lock in gains while capturing further downside potential in the pair. To recap, we initiated this trade on 6 August, fading the spike in the cross observed following the Colombian central bank’s dovish surprises (a decision not to hike and an accumulation of international reserves). We expected the cross to remain driven by external forces, pointing toward further carry-seeking behavior in FX markets given the lack of catalysts for a significant USD rally (see FX: Short CLPCOP, dated 6 August).
