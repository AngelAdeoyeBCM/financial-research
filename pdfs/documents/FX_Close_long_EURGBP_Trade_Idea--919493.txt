# FX: Close long EURGBP

We have been long EURGBP since 9 December last year to reflect our bullish year-ahead outlook for the cross (see FX: Buying the dip in EURGBP). This outlook is predicated on ongoing political uncertainty in the UK and scope for further Bank of England rate cuts (we see a terminal rate of 3% being reached in H1 2027). Overnight results from the Gorton and Denton by-election showed a Green party victory and the Labour party coming third. This result presents fresh pressure on Keir Starmer and can equate to ongoing political risk premium in the GBP. EURGBP has subsequently rallied. From here though, we do not see any near-term catalysts to push EURGBP higher and the trade is negative carry. We therefore choose to move to the sidelines and exit the trade for a small loss (-0.19%; -USD19k). We remain long EURGBP delta via a long SX5E higher, EURGBP higher dual digital (see: Hybrid insights: SX5E and EURGBP higher, dated 29 January). Trade idea: Close long EURGBP at 0.87542 (GMT 08:46) for a 0.19% loss (-USD19k; notional: 10mn).
