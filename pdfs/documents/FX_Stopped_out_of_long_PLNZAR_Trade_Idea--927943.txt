# FX: Stopped out of long PLNZAR

. 10/07/2026 1

RKE. We exit our long PLNZAR trade idea at 4. 28 (−1.8% for a loss of USD180k), hitting our stop-loss after a dovish press conference on Thursday from NBP governor Adam Glapiński. Technicals had prompted the trade, with our positioning and valuation indicators showing stretched levels (see FX: Long PLNZAR on stretched technicals, dated 1 July). We also felt divergence between the Polish and South African central banks was well priced in. However, Glapinski has now clearly opened the door for a September rate cut and it was notable that he chose not to talk down the possible negative impact on the currency. We therefore no longer think 4.30 will remain a sticky level for EURPLN and believe the zloty may continue to underperform.
