# FX: Take profit on short AUDZAR

We take profit on the trade idea we opened in FX: Short AUDZAR on 7 May, for a 1.49% gain (USD 149k). We initiated the trade as a tactical position given that both positioning and valuations appeared stretched in the cross. Despite the continued volatility in equites and energy prices, the cross has moved lower, in line with what valuations and positioning suggested. With long positioning in the cross now trimmed (Figure 1) and valuations on our STEER model no longer compelling (Figure 2), we deem it prudent to take profit at these levels.
