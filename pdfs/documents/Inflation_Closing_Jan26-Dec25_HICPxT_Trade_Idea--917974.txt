# Inflation: Closing Jan26-Dec25 HICPxT

. 04/02/2026 1

RKE. We close our Jan26-Dec25 HICPxT spread trade at −26.9, 2.1bp away from entry and for a total profit of EUR60k. German January inflation showed a limited pass-through from VAT cuts, as we had anticipated, in part causing a modest upside surprise to consensus in the reading. French data fell short of our expectations, however, creating a headwind for the trade we opened in EU HICPxT technically offer Dec25, bid Jan26 on 31 March 2025. Josha van Spronsen, PhD, Inflation Strategist, Europe | MAR | BNP Paribas London Branch
