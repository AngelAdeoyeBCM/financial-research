# EM rates: Close COP basis payer

Our COP basis payer position has touched the stop-loss level, and we therefore exit the trade and book a loss of 18bp (USD92k). A shrinkage in USD inventory, caused by the government’s aggressive buying of about USD2.5bn in recent weeks to close the TRS deal, has applied downward pressure to the COP basis. Meanwhile, increased AFP hedging has added additional pressure to the basis curve. Nevertheless, over a multi-month horizon we still expect the 2y tenor basis to drift higher as local banks continue to seek external financing. To recap, we entered the trade on 28 January to capture an expected build-up of domestic USD inventory in Colombia, fueled by government USD monetisation, Coltes inflows, corporate asset liquidations ahead of the tax season, and local banks’ appetite for synthetic funding (see EM rates: Entering a COP basis payer).
