# EGBs: Closing 30y BTP–DSL tightener

. 23/03/2026 1

RKE. We close our long-standing 30y BTP–DSL tightener, opened in EGBs: Long 30y BTP vs short 30y DSL on 8 July, for a P&L of 1.6bp or EUR+32k (including accumulated positive carry). Prior to the conflict, the trade was previously deep in the money with over 30bp of accumulated P&L. In Europe: Energy shock prompts market paradigm shift, dated 20 March 2026, we argued that the developments in the Middle East have resulted in a paradigm shift, invalidating many of the assumptions we had embedded in our previous forecasts for spreads, resulting in an outlook with lower real GDP growth, ECB rate hikes, higher volatility and a regime shift away from carry trades. We see risks skewed towards a further escalation, which could see BTP spreads widen even further from here. Considering all this, we close the trade now.
