# EM rates: Taking profit on 1s5s THOR steepener after hitting target

We close our 24-Jun-26, 1s5s Thai Overnight Repurchase Rate (THOR) steepener trade idea at 75bp for a profit of 22bp (USD440k), as the position has reached our target (see EM rates: Green shoots in Thailand; pay 1s5s, dated 24 June). We initiated this position on our expectation that Thai economic growth would be supported by a surge in FDI into high-value-added sectors such as data centres, cloud services and electric vehicles. We anticipated steepening pressure on the curve, driven by the widespread view that the Bank of Thailand would remain on hold amid weak domestic consumption, coupled with a deteriorating fiscal outlook as the debt-to-GDP ratio trends towards the 70% ceiling. The trade has performed in line with our expectations. Credit growth in Thailand has continued to accelerate, driven by lending to businesses (Figure 1). Additionally, Q2 GDP growth exceeded market consensus, which was acknowledged by the BoT during the most recent MPC meeting. And despite rising inflation, the BoT has indeed maintained a neutral stance on policy rates. Globally, elevated oil prices and a hawkish Federal Reserve have also put upward pressure on yields, with global as well as domestic long-end yields rising substantially. Consequently, our 24-Jun-26, 1s5s THOR steepener trade idea has reached its target (Figure 2). We maintain a steepener bias, but think it is prudent to take profit at this stage and re-enter at more attractive levels. We close the position at 75bp for a profit of 22bp (USD440k), including carry and rolldown.
