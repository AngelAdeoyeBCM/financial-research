# EM rates: Extending target on long 20y KTB vs short 20y UST

We extend our target on the long position we have been running in long-end KTBs against USTs, as South Korea’s and the US’s fiscal positions have diverged (see EM rates: Long 20y KTB vs UST on diverging fiscal outlook, dated 26 August). The trade has performed in line with our expectations, with long-end KTBs outperforming amid a sustained selloff in US rates (Figure 1). In South Korea, the latest announced budget for 2027 projects the country’s overall fiscal position to improve significantly over the coming years (see South Korea: Expansionary fiscal stance to continue, dated 2 September). We expect KTB outperformance to continue, supported by FTSE World Government Bond Index inflows over the next two months and the prospect of the Ministry of Economy and Finance announcing a reduction in long-end KTB supply towards the end of 2026. As the spread approaches our original target of −100bp, we extend our target to −130bp while trailing our stop-loss level to −70bp.
