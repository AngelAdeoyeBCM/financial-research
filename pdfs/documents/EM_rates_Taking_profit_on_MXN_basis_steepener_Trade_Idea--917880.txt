# EM rates: Taking profit on MXN basis steepener

As the MXN basis steepner position has moved in our favor, we are exiting the trade and booking a profit of 7.5bp (USD75k). Recent issuance of external debt by Mexican local corporates and an uptick in hedging that capitalizes on USDMXN weakness have driven the longer-tenor basis sharply higher, injecting steepening pressure into the curve. At the same time, front-end funding in Mexico has stabilized, limiting short-term downside risk. Consequently, we are locking in profits by closing out now. To recap, we entered the trade on 14 January to position for the expected persistence of downward bias in the basis, supported by the typical seasonal dip in remittance inflows. We also recognized that potentially sizable local external debt issuance in January could reduce downside pressure on the longer-end basis (see Mexico: Entering an MXN basis steepener trade).
