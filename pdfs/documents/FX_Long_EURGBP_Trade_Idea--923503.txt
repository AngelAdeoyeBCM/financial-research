# FX: Long EURGBP

Joshua Wilcock, G10 FX Strategist | Alex Jekov, Head of G10 FX Strategy, Europe | Dani Stoilova, Europe Economist | BNP Paribas London Branch | Ishan Gurnani, G10 FX Strategist | BNP Paribas Securities Corp. Sources: BNP Paribas, Macrobond Fig. 1: We do not see a discount currently embedded in GBP to reflect ongoing political uncertainty We go short GBP, via long EURGBP ahead of the 7 May local elections. This is in addition to our existing EURGBP higher/ SX5E higher dual digital trade idea (see Hybrid insights: SX5E and EURGBP higher, dated 29 January). After a period of resilience amid the energy shock, we think valuations do not reflect ongoing political uncertainty (Figure 1) and the FX market is positioned flat EURGBP according to the BNP Paribas FX Positioning Tracker (Figure 2). Given FX valuations and positioning, we therefore think GBP is vulnerable to an election outcome seen as negative for GBP. A change of leadership presents the tail risk of a shift to a less fiscally prudent government (see UK politics: Succession scenarios, dated 13 February). Past episodes of political risk premium being embedded in the GBP have been sharp. Recent examples include fiscal and political concerns in the lead-up to November’s budget and February’s renewed pressure on the prime minister over Peter Mandelson’s appointment as US ambassador. In these two episodes, EURGBP rallied more than 2% and went above fair value (Figure 1). We think rate differentials have been traded by the market as a source of support for the GBP during the energy shock (see FX and energy Q&A: Strait answers, dated 12 March). That said we do not foresee rate differentials as a durable source of support for the GBP, as it is also consistent with weaker UK growth amid the energy shock.

## KEY MESSAGES

We think risk-reward favours adding EURGBP longs ahead of the UK local elections on 7 May. EURGBP is trading beneath its short-term fair value, implying no political risk premium is imbedded into the currency. Rate differentials are not a durable source of support for the GBP, in our view, considering UK growth weakness amid the energy shock. Trade details: long EURGBP at 0.86418 (BST 12:25).
