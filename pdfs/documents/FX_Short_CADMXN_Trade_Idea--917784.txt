# FX: Short CADMXN

. 02/02/2026 1

RKE.

## KEY MESSAGES

- We see a favorable asymmetry of risk for the MXN relative to the CAD, as trade negotiations with the US are likely to weigh on Canada more than on Mexico.
- Strong US data create a supportive backdrop for carry, from which the MXN can benefit in the Latam complex.
- Trade idea: Short CADMXN Entry 12.7266; target 12 stop-loss 13.15; total allocation USD5mn. Top up: 5mn at 12.95. We initiate a short CADMXN trade to capture our view of a favor able asymmetry of risk for the MXN relative to the CAD as negotiations on the US–Mexico–Canada trade agreement advance, coupled with further room for carry trades to perform. The pullback in the cross following last week’s price action creates a favorable entry level, in our view. Even though the US Trade Representative’s statement suggests that USMCA renewal is in the best interest of all parties, we see scope for market volatility in the coming months due to uncertainty and the possibility that the US negotiating position might include suggesting breaking up the treaty or bilateral deals. This upcoming period of uncertainty would likely weigh on Canada more than Mexico, given the apparent difference in political favor from the US administration. For instance, we note two contrasting Truth Social posts from President Donald Trump last week, one discussing a “productive telephone conversation” with President Claudia Sheinbaum that went “extremely well”, and the other raising the prospect of 50% US import tariffs on Canadian aircraft. This tone is consistent with what the administration has said thus far and what we think may remain a key difference between the US trading partners during the ongoing USMCA negotiations. Despite last Friday’s wash-out of long metals and short USD positions following the confirmation of Kevin Wash as Fed chair, we expect the continuation of a range-bound USD against G10, which would support carry positions (CADMXN offers 5% on an annualized basis). Likewise, we expect the US cyclical position to remain strong, supporting the MXN through the trade and remittances channels. Finally, we note that in the next months the MXN could take the lead as a Latam carry proxy due to volatility related to the political cycles in Brazil and Colombia.
