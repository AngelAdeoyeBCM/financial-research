# EM rates: Take profit on long KTB position, funded via FX implied yields

We close our long KTB position (funded via FX implied yields) for a profit of USD165.4k (8.5bp). We established this carry trade over a year ago when implied yields were low relative to bond yields (see EM rates: Long 2y KTB with FX implied funding, dated 6 June 2025). In May, we took advantage of a rally in KRW rates to roll our implied yield funding to hedge the bond’s remaining duration (see EM rates: Take profit on KRW 1y receiver, roll paid position on FX implied yields, dated 26 May 2026). Over the last year, although KTB yields have increased, the carry and the upward movement in FX implied yields have resulted in a net profit for the trade. The free cash flow for Korean exporters has risen rapidly over the past few months, leading to an increase in USD liquidity onshore, which in turn has driven the cross-currency basis and FX implied yields higher in recent weeks (Figure 1). As the positive carry has diminished at current levels due to the move in FX implied yields (Figure 2), we prefer to close the position. We exited the bond at 3.43% and now unwind the remaining FX implied position at 3.34%, capturing a total profit of USD165.4k on the trade idea, including carry and rolldown.
