# EGBs: Closing 2y OAT–SPGB tightener

We close our 2y OAT –SPGB tightener, entered on 14 May for a small loss. With tensions in the Middle East reigniting, Brent crude has breached the USD100 /bbl level, sending front-end rates higher. The sudden volatility has resulted in the 2y OAT–SPGB tightener hitting its stop-loss of 12bp. The positive carry harvested on the trade (+1.75bp) has offset more than half the mark-to-market loss (−3.2bp), resulting in a net P&L impact of −1.45bp or −EUR43k. We keep our core trade, namely our short 10y FRA in the ITA-FRA-GER credit fly from 8 May.
