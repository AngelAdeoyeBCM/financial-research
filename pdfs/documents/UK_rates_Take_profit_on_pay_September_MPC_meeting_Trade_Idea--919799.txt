# UK rates: Take profit on pay September MPC meeting

. 03/03/2026 1

RKE. We take profit on the trade idea we opened in UK politics: Succession scenarios on 13 February as we hit our take-profit level at the open this morning, for a profit of 18bp or 450k (25k/01). The geopolitical escalation over the weekend ha s seen oil and gas prices surge. In turn, inflation breakevens have repriced sharply higher, leading to an erosion of rates cuts still priced in as of last Friday, across G4. The front-end move has been notably strong, with the March Bank of England MPC meeting now priced at just 3bp, from a peak of 21.6bp last Friday (we booked profit on our March MPC received position mid-February). While we would have preferred to hold onto our pay Sep MPC meeting for a prolonged period, as we see more room for an upward repricing in oil and gas, our trade hit its target at the open this morning after another sharp move in excess of 10bp. Only 63% of a cut is now priced by September (15.8bp), cumulatively, with the Sep MPC meeting at 3.57% at the time of writing, nearly 10bp higher than our target. Take profit on pay September MPC. Entry: 3.30%. Closed at target: 3.48%. Profit: 18bp or 450k (25k/01).
