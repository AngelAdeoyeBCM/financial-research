# FX: Take profit on short MXNCOP for 6.5% gain

We exit our short MXNCOP NDF position at 172.93 (entry: 184.88), booking a gain of 6.5% (USD650k). We exit the position, as it is likely that the cross is consolidating after its relevant move observed in the past few weeks. On the MXN side, the market has already incorporated the probability of Banxico making an additional fine-tuning cut in the coming months. Nevertheless, our base scenario remains that Banxico will not deliver on such signaling. Regarding the COP, even though we still see room for the currency to outperform on the back of the government’s USD monetization and high carry, current levels suggest limited room for a rally in the short term. To recap, we initiated this trade idea on 9 September as we expected the start of the Fed hiking cycle coupled with a dovish Banxico to weigh on the MXN. We were also expecting further COP outperformance amid a favorable external environment and expectations of strong government USD inflows (see FX: Short MXNCOP, dated 9 September).
