# FX: Close short CNHTWD

. 09/06/2026 1

RKE. We close the trade idea we initiated in FX: Tactical short CNHTWD on 20 April for a modest loss of 0.24% (USD24.3k out of USD10mn notional). We opened the tactical position expecting the TWD to outperform, driven by foreign equity inflows, Taiwanese policymakers’ efforts to curb inflation and favourable seasonality. We used the CNH as a funder, as we anticipated the People’s Bank of China will smooth the fall in USDRMB spot and the rise in the RMB index. The trade performed in line with our expectations initially. However, stronger USD boosted the CNH, as Chinese exporters rushed to settle on rebound, a risk we had highlighted in the note. For the TWD, several headwinds emerged: tech giants missed earnings expectations, concerns grew about an earlier peak in the chip super‐cycle and expectations of Fed tightening rose. These sparked a correction in tech equities, triggering outflows from TWSE stocks and adding pressure on the currency. This has made the TWD an average performer compared to its Asian peers. We see further risks as investors rotate to higher-USD trades (see FX: The USD and rate hikes, dated 5 June). We still believe that the CNH will outperform its Asian peers in a higher-USD environment, anchored by exporters’ settlement demand and prevailing RMB appreciation expectations. We think that the PBoC is comfortable with the current appreciation pace, with the fixing trending downward. On the other hand, we see a modest near‐term upside for USDTWD amid a global USD recovery and Taiwanese authorities’ measures to contain two-way volatility.
