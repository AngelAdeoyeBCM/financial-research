# DM rates: Stop out of Dec 26–27 Sonia flattener ## ## ## ## ## ## ## ## ## ## ## ## ## ## ## ## TRADE IDEA

## Use the Table of Contents below for a long note that is split into sections with colour-coded banners at the top of each page from p.2 onwards. We entered a Dec 26-Dec 27 Sonia flattener on 7 May, anticipating that markets would start pricing in our expected 2027 Bank of England rate cuts (see UK rates: Enter Dec 26–27 Sonia flattener). Our view remains unchanged: we expect one more hike this year and three cuts next year to a 3.50% terminal rate. However, both geopolitical and UK political developments have been more volatile than expected. Renewed Middle East tensions recently have driven the trade to its stop-loss, as markets have failed to price in our expected cuts for next year. Trade idea: Stop out of Dec 26–27 at 10bp. Entry: −15bp. P&L: −25bp (GBP-500k). In UK rates: Enter Dec 26–27 Sonia flattener, dated 7 May, we entered a Dec 26-Dec 27 Sonia flattener to express our view that markets would start to price in the BoE cuts that we expect in 2027. Although our view has not changed (we still expect one more hike this year and three cuts next year to a terminal rate of 3.50%), the developments in both the geopolitical and UK political space has been more volatile than expected. Recently, the renewed tensions in the Middle-East has driven the trade to its stop, with markets failing to price in the cuts that we expect next year. Stop out on Dec 26–27 at 10bp. Entry: -15bps. P&L: -25bps.
