# FX: Closing out long INRIDR

. 04/03/2026 1

RKE. The long INRIDR 6m NDF trade idea we opened in FX: Tariff and trade tailwinds for INR on 4 February has reached its stop–loss and we close it for a loss of 1.56%. We initiated the trade to express our medium-term bullish view on the INR. We felt that India’s trade headwinds were abating, and could become tailwinds for the economy over the medium term, with trade deals being struck with the US, EU and other countries. Economic data had also shown signs of improvement. Given a resilient global backdrop, that potentially favoured carry strategies, we saw scope for portfolio inflows into INR. We also thought seasonal repatriation by exporters ahead of the tax-year end in March could provide additional support to the currency. However, in the wake of conflict in the Middle East, oil prices have risen sharply, as has volatility, prompting investors to broadly de-risk. As India is a large oil importer – and with the BNP Paribas Global FX Positioning Tracker showing investors holding long INR and short IDR exposure into last weekend’s escalation – the cross has declined. We have now reached the stop –loss level on our trade idea, so we close out the position at 181.719 (USDINR 6Aug26 NDF: 93.265, USDIDR 6Aug26 NDF: 16948, 20:44 GMT+8) for a loss of 1.56% (USD 156k). Should the risk environment stabilise, and oil prices moderate, we would be inclined to re-add bullish INR exposure to our model portfolio.
