# AUD rates: Close paid Aug RBA; remain received in AUD 2y2y vs USD 2y2y

We close our trade idea to pay the August RBA meeting at 4.36% for a loss of 9.75bp (USD195k). This trade was caught on the wrong side of the move following the Q2 CPI downside surprise (see AUD rates: Pay June/Aug RBA, dated 21 May). Earlier this month, we pivoted from our long-held paid bias in AUD rates to a received bias, anticipating that the August SoMP would contain dovish undertones. Today's meeting validated our view. The RBA voted unanimously to keep rates on hold, and while RBA Governor Bullock maintained a hawkish rhetoric, the August SoMP forecasted a swifter move of the trimmed-mean inflation towards the target midpoint and acknowledged softening in the labour and housing markets. We expect markets to increasingly converge towards the view that the RBA is done with hiking rates, and we remain comfortable running our received position in AUD 2y2y, hedged with a paid position in USD 2y2y (see AUD rates: Shifting to a received bias – receive AUD 2y2y vs USD 2y2y payer, dated 7 Aug).
