# FX: Taking 3% profit on short CLPCOP

We take profit on our short CLPCOP NDF position at 3.4145, booking a gain of 3.1% (USD310k). Last week we trailed the stop-loss on the short position to beyond the entry level (see FX: Adjusting levels for our short CLPCOP position). CLPCOP has since hit that revised level following recent upward momentum fueled by declining oil prices. We believe the pair retains downside potential over a multi-month horizon – supported by the significant Chile–Colombia interest-rate spread and potential continued carry-seeking behavior. To recap, we initiated this trade on 6 August to fade the cross spike triggered by the Colombian central bank’s dovish surprises (see FX: Short CLPCOP).
