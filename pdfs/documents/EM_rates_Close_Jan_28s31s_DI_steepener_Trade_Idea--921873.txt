# EM rates: Close Jan 28s31s DI steepener

We are exiting the Jan 28s31s DI steepener we entered on 19 March, booking a loss of 27.5bp (USD275k). The 31s DI has outperformed the 28s DI amid the broader sell-off pressure in global fixed income. Although Brazil’s easing cycle and the inverted curve still make a DI steepener attractive, in our view, we exit the position because of persistent uncertainty over the Middle-East backdrop. When we opened the trade idea, we believed a combination of external drivers (the energy price shock) and domestic factors (the start of the BCB’s easing cycle) would support a steeper curve, and that the 28s31s spread would offer an attractive entry point after a significant flattening move (see EM rates: Entering Jan 28s31s DI steepener).
