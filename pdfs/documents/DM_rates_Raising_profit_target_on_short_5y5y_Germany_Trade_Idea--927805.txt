# DM rates: Raising profit target on short 5y5y Germany

Camille de Courcel, Head of DM Rates Strategy and Economics, Europe | MAR | Anubhav Lamichhane, G10 Rates Strategist, Europe | MAR | Stev en Montgomery, G10 Rates Strategist, Europe | MAR | Katherine Yoon, Europe Rates Strategist | MAR | BNP Paribas London Branch | Andrea Fiorav anti, Europe Rates Strategist | BNP Paribas Portugal Branch We added to our short 5y5y Germany trade idea at 3.098% on 30 June, after the downward surprise to eurozone inflation spurred another leg to the rates rally; a rally that gained momentum in the week preceding quarter-end, due to substantial quarter-end rebalancing flows (see DM rates: Adding to our short 5y5y Germany). We argued that, once quarter-end over, there would be a lack of catalysts to maintain bond valuations at such expensive levels. Bond supply has resumed at a decent level, especially in Germany where July is the 2nd heaviest month of the year after January, on a net term basis. This, coupled with heightened Middle East tensions, has resulted in us quickly approaching the 3.40% profit target we had set for the second leg of our trade (currently at 3.36%). Hence, we raise the target to 3.50% – the original target for our trade in DM rates: Narrow term premium make shorts appealing – and add a stop-win at 3.30%, 20bp away from entry. We also raise the stop on the first leg of our trade idea to its entry level of 3.23%.
