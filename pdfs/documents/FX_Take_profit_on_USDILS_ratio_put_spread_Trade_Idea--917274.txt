# FX: Take profit on USDILS ratio put spread

. 26/01/2026 1

RKE. We take USD55k profit on our long 3m (25-Feb) USDILS 3.21-3.15 put spread in USD15mn x USD22.5mn, which we opened on 25 November 2025 in FX: Initiating long USDILS ratio put spread. USDILS is currently close to four-year lows and long positioning in ILS FX has increased since we opened the trade idea. However, at this level in spot the position has small positive USDILS delta, and we deem it prudent to take profit. More broadly, we think the fundamentals will remain supportive for ILS, as institutional investors’ FX hedge ratios have room to rise, repatriation flows continue and non-resident investment is picking up (see FX: Low FX hedge ratios to support the ILS, dated 7 May 2025). Trade details: Close 3m (25-Feb) USDILS 3.21-3.15 put spread in USD15mn x USD22.5mn (66.6bp premium, 3.1300 spot ref).
