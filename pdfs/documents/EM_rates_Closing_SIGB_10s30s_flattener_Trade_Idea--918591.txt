# EM rates: Closing SIGB 10s30s flattener

Sources: Bloomberg, Macrobond, BNP Paribas Fig. 1: 10s30s seasonality in SIGB curve did not play out in 2025 Chandresh Jain, EM Asia FX and Rates Strategist | MAR | Joey Li, Markets Analyst, APAC | BNP Paribas Singapore Branch. We close our SIGB 10s30s flattener trade idea (see EM rates: Enter a 10s30s SIGB flattener, dated 26 September) for a 16.5bp loss, as it has reached our stop. We initiated the position after the Monetary Authority of Singapore’s 50y SIGB auction in September. Seasonality analysis suggested that the long-end part of the SIGB curve could flatten, as MAS usually doesn’t auction super-long bonds in Q4 and Q1. While the curve flattened somewhat heading into the auction and in the days after, the broader flattening move in 10s30s did not play out as we had expected (see Figure 1). As such, we close our position (long 30y SIGB 3.25% June 2054 and short 10y SIGB 2.75% March 2035) at 27.5bp for a 16.5bp loss (USD330k).
