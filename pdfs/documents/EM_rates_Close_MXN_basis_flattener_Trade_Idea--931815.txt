# EM rates: Close MXN basis flattener

We are closing our MXN basis flattener (U26-2y vs. U26-10y) at 2.5bp, booking a loss of 3bp (USD60k). The long-end MXN basis has struggled to decline due to a resilient MXN supported by robust US cyclical position through remittances and trade flows. While we anticipate that a potential MXN sell-off, prompted by upcoming Fed hikes, could benefit this position by compressing the long-end MXN basis and flattening the curve, we have opted to close our position rather than roll it. This decision is driven by the risk of seasonal upside pressure on the long-end basis, which is likely to stem from increased local external debt issuance and the associated hedging flows to pay the long-end basis Consequently, we are maintaining a short MXNCOP position to express our relatively bearish outlook on the MXN, rather than utilizing the MXN basis curve. To recap, we initiated this trade on 3 June to capture rising MXN cash demand; additionally, the position provides exposure to a stronger USDMXN and lower hedging activity, which could compress the long-end basis (see EM rates: Entering an MXN basis flattener trade).
