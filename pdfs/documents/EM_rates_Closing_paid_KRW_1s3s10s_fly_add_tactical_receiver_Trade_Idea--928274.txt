# EM rates: Closing paid KRW 1s3s10s fly; add tactical receiver

Chandresh Jain, EM Asia FX and Rates Strategist | MAR | Joey Li, FX and Rates Strategist, APAC | BNP Paribas Singapore Branch | Jeeho Yoon, Senior South Korea and Taiwan Economist | BNP Paribas SA Seoul Branch | Franco Hsu, APAC Markets Strategist | BNP Paribas Hong Kong Branch | Jeeho Yoon is employed as an Economist by BNP Paribas Seoul Branch and is authorised to provide views on South Korean macroeconomic topics only We close our paid position in KRW 18-May-26, 1s3s10s fly at 33bp for a profit of 1bp (USD20k), see South Korea: More reasons to hike rates, dated 18 May. We believe there may be scope for KRW rates to rally following the Bank of Korea’s (BoK) press conference today. Some of the external factors that were driving KRW rates higher are relaxing: US rates have declined following soft CPI and PPI numbers, and USDKRW has dropped significantly from its peak over the last two weeks, aided by FX hedge unwind flows from equity investors (see FX: Why USDKRW is lower when equities are selling off, dated 8 July) and flows relating to ADR listing. We believe market positioning is paid, as markets are worried about back-to-back rate hikes from the BoK. As some of these pressures soften, the BoK may not signal back-to-back hikes today, which could trigger a rally in KRW rates. Although we maintain our view that the fundamental drivers of strong growth in South Korea remain intact, the market is still pricing 5 rate hikes from the BoK, which we think is excessive versus our base case of 2 rate hikes. Tactically, we think it makes sense to add a small receiver now – we add a received position in M27, 1y in USD10k at 4.13%, targeting 3.90% and with a stop at 4.28%. Carry and roll down: 2bp/month.
