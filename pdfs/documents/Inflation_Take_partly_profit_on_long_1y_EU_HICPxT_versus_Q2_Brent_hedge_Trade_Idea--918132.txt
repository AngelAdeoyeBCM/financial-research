# Inflation: Take partly profit on long 1y EU HICPxT versus Q2 Brent hedge

. 06/02/2026 1

RKE. We reduce our long 1y EU HICPxT exposure by 50%, reducing to EUR20k/bp, at 179.3bp, 12.3bp above our entry level, for a profit of EUR245k. We had initiated the idea on 12 January 2026 (see Inflation: Long 1y EU HICPxT vs Q2 2026 Brent hedge). We believe today’s discussions reduce the probability of a crude pricing blow out in the near-term. However, we do not think that discussions between the US and Iran will fully assuage tensions, see Iran scenarios: Power vacuum bigger energy worry than Hormuz constraint, dated 23 January, for more detail. Therefore, we retain half the exposure to capture potential upside from further escalation. We tactically take partly profit on our idea, as the 1y has repriced considerably and held up despite last week’s weaker performance of Brent crude oil. Josha van Spronsen, PhD, Inflation Strategist, Europe | MAR | Aldo Spanjer PhD, Head of Energy Strategy | MAR | BNP Paribas London Branch.
