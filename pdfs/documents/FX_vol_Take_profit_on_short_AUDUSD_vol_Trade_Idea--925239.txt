# FX vol: Take profit on short AUDUSD vol

. 29/05/2026 1

B N P PA GL OB ET S A PP We close the trade idea we opened in FX vol: Short AUDUSD vol as May’s bearish seasonality looms on 22 April for a profit of 11.5bp (41.5bp versus 30bp entry; 0.7163 spot ref). The trade positioned for bearish seasonality in May; AUDUSD 3m implied vol has fallen by 1.2pp since trade inception. However, we do not find significant bearish seasonality for AUDUSD in the month of June, and the level of 3m implied vol is now near 8% (close to the post-2020 low, Figure 1). In addition, the implied/realised spread indicat es that vol risk premium has disappeared (Figure 2). In a US–Iran de-escalation scenario, we think risky assets could perform strongly, and vol would likely fall further as risk premium is taken out of vol surfaces. But the short-vol exposure on the EKO structure we used has fallen and it is now increasingly long-AUDUSD delta as the expiry date approaches. As AUD remains one of the longest-positioned cu rrencies according to our FX positioning tracker, we prefer to own under-owned risky currencies for a de-escalation outcome and are short AUDZAR delta for this scenario.
