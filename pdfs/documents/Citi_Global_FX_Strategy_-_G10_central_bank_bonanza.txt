# Global FX Strategy G10 central bank bonanza

## CITI’S TAKE

We remain bearish EUR into a busy central bank week featuring the Fed and ECB. Elsewhere, the BoC is most interesting as Citi Economics does not expect a hike (~21bps priced) and with USDCAD at the pivotal 1.40 area. n | USD/Fed Citi Economics preview: US Economics - FOMC Preview - No hike, no guidance Fed to leave rates unchanged this week. Market pricing sits at ~ 3bps, and while this is far from the hawkish premium into the July meeting, an unchanged policy rate could still see modest knee-jerk USD selling as that premium goes unrealized. In that scenario, we would be USD dip buyers into the presser. CPI/PPI printed above expectations and PCE inflation – released one day after the meeting, but which Fed officials will know with reasonable certainty – should accelerate to 3.6% YoY. Upcoming GDP data is also expected to remain solid and energy prices rose over the intermeeting period as the US/Iran conflict remains unresolved. These themes should push the FOMC’s discussion in a more hawkish direction, reflecting the “changing balance of risks” and “risk management” cited in the Minutes. We think Chair Warsh will capture this sentiment in his remarks, though would also watch how he responds to questions on

1. financial conditions in the context of the recent AI-driven selloff and
2. why the Fed didn’t hike at this meeting. The setup is mixed for USD. Citi’s FX positioning indicators suggest leveraged investors are only modestly long USD (+1.5 out of 10), though our fair value model indicates moderate DXY overvaluation vs relative rates (+0.9%). We also expect follow-through will be sensitive to large cap tech earnings later that day, where USD has been vulnerable amid its heightened sensitivity to the AI trade. Coupled with month-end rebalancing and FX hedging, USD price action could get choppy, but we stay bearish EURUSD as relative rates could move back in favor of USD. Figure 1. Markets price 24bps for the Fed into year-end Figure 2. Investors are long USD into FOMC EUR/ECB Citi Economics preview: Economics - ECB: Changing Times? ECB to leave rates unchanged this week. The backdrop into the meeting is one where energy prices have moved closer to the ECB’s ‘Severe’ scenarios, and the outlook for Eurozone inflation has worsened, but OAT-Bund spreads have widened significantly. We think the market will focus primarily on how President Lagarde navigates questions on widening spreads, including how she characterizes the conditional backstops available. Such backstops, in our view, likely prove ineffective at solving the root causes of the spread widening (European Economics - The Causes and Consequences of France’s Debt Challenge). On net, we think this backdrop makes a hawkish surprise less likely. Citi Rates Strategy similarly flags this may mean that ECB pricing is at a tipping point, and like receiving Dec26 ECB €STR (spot ref: 2.65%, 8:58 AM ET) on the premise OAT volatility ultimately caps the potential for future ECB hikes. We have been bearish EUR after flagging growing European-centric tail risks, and our FX flows point to an acceleration of leveraged EUR selling in recent weeks. We remain short EURNOK (spot ref: 10.4535, 8:58 AM ET) as one of our preferred bearish EUR expressions that would also benefit from US/Iran re-escalation. EURGBP is another interesting cross with the UK Autumn Budget also this week. A non-event budget could catalyze renewed foreign inflows into gilts and would offer another tailwind for EURGBP downside (UK Multi-Asset - UK: Market-Friendly Budget, Nuanced Equity Impact). The focus, in our view, will be whether Governor Ueda lays the groundwork for the next rate hike ahead of the December meeting. Given recent corrections in both US and Japanese equity markets, however, we do not think his comments will be excessively hawkish this time. We believe the USDJPY is likely to remain in the ¥155/$–¥160/$ range near term. However, the currency policy of the Japanese MoF is clearly becoming more aggressive. We would expect the next round of intervention to buy the JPY if the USDJPY tops ¥160/$, with the aim of pushing it below ¥155/$. CAD staged an impressive rally against USD over the past week with the pair returning briefly breaking 1.40. This move has mostly been in the context of the leveraged CAD short covering, meaning positioning now looks cleaner into this week’s meeting. The 1.3885-1.40 area has been pivotal for USDCAD since 2022, and we would expect support to hold in the event the BoC leaves rates unchanged. Any knee-jerk CAD selling could moderate over the presser, however, as Governor Macklem is still expected to guide towards conditional rate hikes. More interesting to us would be a scenario where the BoC hikes but CAD fails to stage a meaningful rally. Figure 7. Markets price 38bps for the BoC into year-end Figure 8.
