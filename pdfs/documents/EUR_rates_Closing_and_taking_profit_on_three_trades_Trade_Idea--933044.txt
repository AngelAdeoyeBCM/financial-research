# EUR rates: Closing and taking profit on three trades

We close our long 15 Portugal vs Austria spread tightener trade, initiated on 18 September. The flow driven sell-off in France, coupled with the risk-off moves in EGBs, has resulted in a significant widening of Portugal against Austria. The fiscal and economic fundamentals still justify a convergence of the Portugal-Austria spread over the long term, we think. However, widening EGB spreads near term and the moves in OATs act as a headwind for the trade. Close long 15 POR-AUS at the stop of 36.5bp for a P&L of -9.9bp on EUR-396k including carry. We take profit on the 5s30s SPGB-GER flattener we initiated on 20 May. The exceptional widening at the front-end of the BTP and OAT curve yesterday, which appears symptomatic of stop-out flows given an abs ence of fundamental catalysts, has led to a significant cheapening of 5y SPGB-GER, which we thought looked too rich. With levels almost hitting our target, we take profit on the position. Close 5s30s SPGB-GER flattener at 37.4bp for a profit of 7.49bp or EUR683k including carry. We close the 6m fwd 2s10s ESTR flattener hedge we initiated on 25 September after it reached its stop of 16.5bp, for a P&L of -11.5bp or EUR-287.5k. The risk-off moves, exacerbated by positioning and flow, have resulted in a bid at the front-end, lowering ECB pricing and steepening the curve, despite no resolution to the Middle East war.
