# EM rates: Close receive 1y HKD versus pay 1y USD IRS

. 25/06/2026 1

RKE. The trade idea we opened in EM rates: Receive 1y HKD vs pay 1y USD IRS on 30 March has reached its stop-loss. We close it at 57.7bp for a loss of 19.8bp (USD198k out of USD10k DV01 notional). The trade initially performed as expected, with HKD rates rallying once the March quarter-end seasonality subsided. We kept the trade, believing weak credit demand and modest Southbound equity inflows would keep HKD rates low with a positive roll. However, larger‐than‐expected IPOs and the growing expectation of Fed rate hikes have push ed up short‐term HKD rates. Moreover, additional liquidity pressure from the approaching half-year end eventually triggered the stop-loss. We think front-end liquidity might remain under pressure with still strong IPO offerings. With the USDHKD trading around 7.84, we think ma rket may also be prepositioning for further liquidity tightening, expecting the Hong Kong Monetary Authority to drain liquidity upon reaching 7.85. Ann Jiang, APAC Markets Strategist | MAR | BNP Paribas Hong Kong Branch
