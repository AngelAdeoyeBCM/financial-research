# EGBs: Take profit on short 10y FRA–ITA–GER credit fly

We take profit at 89.4bp on the trade we initiated in EGBs: Short belly in 10y ITA –FRA–GER credit fly on 8 May, for profit of 37bp including carry, or EUR930k. In the recent risk-off move, 10y OAT–Bund spreads have widened from lows of 86bp this Monday to as high as 94.7bp at the time of writing, close to our end-Q4 2026 forecast of 93bp. The recent move is primarily driven by risk-off price action related to the Middle East, in our view, rather than French-specific drivers. Since our entry point of 50.6bp, the fly has moved to 89bp, only 1bp away from our 90bp target. Accordingly, w e take profit on the trade now. S preads could remain vulnerable to a turn in risk sentiment to the benefit of OATs and could provide an opportunity to re-enter shorts at better levels. We retain our view that the 10y OAT –Bund spread will widen to 100bp in Q1 2 027 into the French presidential elections.
