# G10 FX: Take profit on long NZDCAD

. 26/01/2026 1

RKE. We are closing the position that was opened in G10 FX: Entering a long NZDCAD trade on 16 January for a profit of 2.52% (USD252k). This trade idea reflected our expectation for the countries’ economies to diverge and provided a hedge against any revival in trade policy uncertainty. Recent economic data has affirmed that view as a services index for New Zealand showed expansion for the first time in nearly two years, while Canada’s latest Business Outlook Survey remained subdued. Likewise, markets shrugged off an upside surprise in Canadian CPI, while New Zealand’s CPI surprised well above the RBNZ forecast. Market perceptions of a potential escalation in US-Canada trade tensions has also undermined the CAD on crosses. And our long NZDCAD trade has benefitted from the USD being under depreciation pressure, given that the USD and CAD typically correlate mo re than the USD and NZD. Going forward, we would be unsurprised to see the cross extend its move further. NZD positioning is fairly light relative to the high-beta complex (+6 on a +/- 50 scale versus the AUD, SEK and NOK at an average +39), suggesting space for longs to be added. Furthermore, trade uncertainty is likely to remain and we anticipate CAD underperformance as a result. Upcoming data includes labor prints in both Canada and New Zealand. But given the recent repricing, the asymmetry into the events seems weaker than when we entered the position, and so we opt to exit here for a profit. Our bias going forward will remain to view the NZD with a cautious sense of optimism while approaching the CAD from the short side. Ishan Gurnani, G10 FX Strategist | BNP Paribas Securities Corp
