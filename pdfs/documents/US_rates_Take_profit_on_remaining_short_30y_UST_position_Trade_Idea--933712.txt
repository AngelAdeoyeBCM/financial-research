# US rates: Take profit on remaining short 30y UST position

## OUR KEY VIEWS

- We take profit on the remainder of our short 30y UST trade, as we hit our trailing stop (5.45%) – driven by tighter financial conditions and a reversal of negative convexity dynamics.
- We do not think yields have peaked yet, amid unequivocally strong data and absence of fiscal concerns, and we expect there will be opportunities to add shorts again.
- Trade: Close short 30y UST. Entry: 5.36%. Close: 5.45%. Size: $25k/01. Total profit: $725,000. Earlier this week, we realized partial profits on our short 30y UST position (at 5.56%), driven by recent strength in Treasury auctions, a shift toward dovish Fed commentary, and technical indicators suggesting tactical consolidation (see Take partial profits on short 30y UST, dated 13 October). Having reached our revised stop on the remaining half of the short 30y UST position, we have closed the trade, capturing a 9bp profit on the residual holding. A reaction to tighter financial conditions: Consistent with our framework, we would have expected long-end Treasury yields to react to the tightening in financial conditions, especially the jitters in the ‘AI trade’ (see Figure 1). At the same time, as we have noted, the negative convexity dynamics (cheapest-to-deliver switches, mortgage convexity hedging) that drove yields higher sharply were also likely to drive yields lower sharply – which seems to be play ing out. There are also rising expectations that the Treasury might suggest changes to 20y coupon supply at the Treasury refunding questionnaire on Friday, boosting the long end. We do not think those initiatives will work in sustainably lowering yields (see No, cutting 20y Treasury supply will not work, 6 October). We do not think the peak in long-end yields is in yet: Strong upside surprises in September CPI, PPI, and retail sales data (see Figure 2) confirm that the economy remains on a strong footing on growth, and inflation remains sticky as well. At the same time, deficit concerns – which have been notoriously absent – could resurface. We will look to re-enter shorts, unless the tightening in financial conditions proves durable - not our base case view. Trade: Close short 30y UST. Entry: 5.36%. Close: 5.45%. Size: $25k/01.
