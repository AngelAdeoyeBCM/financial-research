# EUR rates: Stopped out of 10s40s steepener

The trade idea we opened in EUR rates: Re-entering steepener and buying long-end volatility on 7 July has reached its stop-loss and we close it for a loss of 14.4bp (EUR 576k). We had entered the trade on the expectation the market would likely move beyond the conflict in the Middle East and once again focus on global and European-specific curve-steepening drivers. However, the recent flare-up in hostilities between the US and Iran forced the market to reconsider the impact a sustained escalation might have on energy prices and front-end rates – notwithstanding the subsequent easing of tensions seen early this week. This bearish front-end move caused our EUR 1y fwd 10s40s steepener to hit its stop-loss.
