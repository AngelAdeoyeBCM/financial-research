# FX: Re-entering long KRW, funded with USD and CNH

Parisha Saimbi, EM Asia FXLM Strategist | MAR | BNP Paribas Singapore Branch | Franco Hsu, APAC Markets Strategist | Ann Jiang, APAC Markets Strategist | BNP Paribas Hong Kong Branch. Sources: Seibro, BNP Paribas Fig. 1: We think the early-May episode could replay again We re-enter long KRW as we believe it stands to benefit in a revived ‘sell-America’ trend, given Korean investors have been overweight US assets over recent years. With a weaker USD and risk-on sentiment still dominant, we believe Korean investors will sell overseas holdings to buy domestic assets, (Figure 1) aided by the recent tax benefits announced in December, and encouraged by a better growth outlook given the chip super-cycle and solid fundamentals among Korean equities. Even if the market turns risk-off, we expect retail outflows to slow, thereby providing offsetting support for the KRW. We have maintained a medium-term bullish view on the KRW and previously published KRW-long trade ideas (see FX: Fading KRW weakness, sell USDKRW 6m NDF, dated 21 November 2025). Following the government’s large-scale FX intervention and policies encouraging repatriation to the KRW – including reshoring capital that has been long-parked in overseas assets – USDKRW fell about 2.5% within one week (FX: Trail stop on short USDKRW, dated 7 January). However, demand for the USD remained robust in early January, and retail investors took advantage to buy on the dip (Figure 1), pushing the pair back to 1470. Consequently, we closed the trade early with a flat P&L (FX: Close out short USDKRW, dated 12 January).

## KEY MESSAGES

We re-enter long KRW and believe the currency stands to benefit from the revived ‘sell-America’ trend. In our view, Korean investors may start reallocating their overseas assets into the domestic market, spurred by a weaker USD and policies that encourage repatriation. Our medium-term bullish KRW view stays intact, reflecting upcoming WGBI inflows, government efforts to guide long-term capital back into Korean assets, and the robust chip super-cycle. USDKRW is close to the authorities’ ‘line in the sand’ once again, making intervention a live risk, in our view. A short CNH position provides a hedge for our short USD exposure in the event of a broader risk-off move, and we think the exporter-driven CNH rally should weaken around mid-February.
