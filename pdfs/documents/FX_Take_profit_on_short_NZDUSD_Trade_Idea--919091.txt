# FX: Take profit on short NZDUSD

. 20/02/2026 1

RKE. We close the trade idea we opened in Hedging a USD bounce via short NZDUSD on 2 February for a small gain of 0.68% (USD34k, spot ref. 0.5970). We initially entered the trade to hedge our portfolio against a USD rebound given historically short levels of positioning (-32, +/-50 scale). NZDUSD downside scanned as attractive at the time given our quantitative signals and the near-term schedule of data releases. The trade sucessfully captured what we argued was an asymmetry into the local employment data release, dovish risks for the RBNZ meeting, as well as US data coming in above consensus expectations. With these catalysts having passed and a limited data calendar up ahead, we therefore opt to lock in profits and exit the position now. Ishan Gurnani, G10 FX Strategist | BNP Paribas Securities Corp.
