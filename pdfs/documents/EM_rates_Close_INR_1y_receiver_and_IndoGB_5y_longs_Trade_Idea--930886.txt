# EM rates: Close INR 1y receiver and IndoGB 5y longs

We close our INR 1y receiver and our long 5y IndoGB trade ideas, as both positions reached our stop-loss levels. INR 1y receiver: We initiated this trade after the Reserve Bank of India (RBI) kept policy rates on hold at its August meeting and adopted a dovish tone. The RBI highlighted a “hazy” growth outlook and focused on India’s super-core inflation, which remains significantly lower than headline or core CPI (see EM rates: Receive INR 1y swap as the probability of no hike is increasing, dated 11 August). At that time, the 1y swap curve was pricing in approximately three rate hikes, given contained depreciation pressures on the INR as the currency was supported by ongoing FCNR measures. The carry was attractive at 7bp/month. Since then, however, three developments have worked against this trade. First, the RBI’s unexpected early closure of the FCNR facility prompted renewed upward pressure on USDINR. Second, the August meeting minutes revealed a more hawkish stance than the post-meeting press conference, with some members signaling potential rate hike (s) toward year-end should inflationary pressures become broad-based. These announcements led to a significant sell-off in INR rates (Figure 1). Finally, unexpected hawkishness from the Fed Chair at the Jackson Hole symposium triggered another round of sell-off in US rates (Figure 2), driving INR rates higher. 5y IndoGB longs: Our long 5y IndoGB position was a tactical play ahead of the 28 Aug SRBI auction, based on the expectation that reduced SRBI reliance would rotate liquidity into the bond market (see Indonesia: A tactical window for IndoGBs under new BI leadership, dated 28 Aug). Over the last few days, re-escalating tensions in the Middle East have led oil prices to rise again and markets to trade risk-off, putting upward pressure on IndoGBs. India: While we maintain our view that the RBI is unlikely to hike more than twice over the next 12 months, we respect our stop-loss level and close our INR 11-Aug-26, 1y NDOIS receiver trade idea at 5.97% for a loss of 16.25bp (USD243.75k), including carry and rolldown. Indonesia: Although a structural rotation from SRBI s towards IndoGBs remains intact in our view, both assets may remain vulnerable in an environment where oil prices are high, and Fed meetings are live. As such, we close our long 5y IndoGB (INDOGB 7 1⁄4 05/15/32) trade idea at 7.10% for a loss of 29.28bp (USD298.6k), including carry and rolldown.
