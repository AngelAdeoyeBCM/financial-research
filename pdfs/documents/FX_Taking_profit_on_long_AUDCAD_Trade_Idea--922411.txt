# FX: Taking profit on long AUDCAD

. 16/04/2026 1

RKE. We take 2.12% (USD212k) profit on our long AUDCAD trade idea we opened on 19 March (see FX: CAD support to wane). The energy price shock from the Middle East-conflict has led the CAD to outperform the G10 complex, partly due to improved relative terms of trade. This had benefited Canada more than other energy exporters, which we thought was unjustified. Hence, we saw scope for the currency to come under pressure. This proved correct. Since the start of April, markets have been trading headlines suggesting potential de-escalation more actively. This has supported our position as markets re-engage with carry trades, of which AUD offers the highest vol-adjusted yield in G10, and given that AUD has one of the highest sensitivities to global risk sentiment. We would also point to CAD acting as a USD proxy as a reason for the price action. Given the scope of the rally and that our risk premium metrics have now moved to stretched levels, we are now wary of some consolidation. As such, now is an opportune time to lock in profits. We still see scope for the cross to extend higher in the medium term, as superannuation funds hedging away USD risk supports AUD structurally and because the CAD is particularly vulnerable to US trade policy. Ishan Gurnani, G10 FX Strategist | BNP Paribas Securities Corp
