# FX: Exit long CLPCOP position

We exit our long CLPCOP position as it has breached the stop-loss level, booking a loss of 3.8% (USD379k). The CLPCOP has dropped sharply in recent sessions, partly due to the upside correction in USDCLP after a more hawkish-than-expected June FOMC meeting. On the COP side, after Colombia’s presidential run-off confirmed market expectations that the opposition candidate – and favourite – Abelardo de la Espriella (ADE) was likely to win, the COP initially experienced further outperformance. After the COP’s sharp rally in past weeks, we see room for a consolidation, now that the market’s expectations have been confirmed. We see possible some short-term reversal of the outperformance against key peers (CLP, BRL, MXN). To recap, we entered the trade on 17 June, expecting the CLP to rebound after its notable underperformance since March, aided by lower oil prices, a strong copper price, and a favorable external backdrop. Meanwhile, stretched technicals in the COP and heavy positioning (reflecting a positive outlook for the Colombian election) had left the currency vulnerable to a technical rebound (see FX: Entering a tactical long CLPCOP position, dated 17 June).
