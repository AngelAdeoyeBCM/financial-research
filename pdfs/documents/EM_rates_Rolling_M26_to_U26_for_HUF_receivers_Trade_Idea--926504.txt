# EM rates: Rolling M26 to U26 for HUF receivers

. 17/06/2026 1

RKE. Yesterday we extended the target for our M26 5y HUF receiver (see EM rates: Extending target on HUF 5y receiver, 16 June). We now roll this trade into U26 5y at −10bp, and we adjust our target and stop levels by the same amount (4.40% target vs 5.70% stop). We roll the 2y leg of our front end flattener (22 Jan 2027 start 3m vs m26 2y) from M26 to U26 as well (at −21bp). We keep the target for the front-end flattener at −40bp and trail the stop to +5bp. We continue to keep our 10y HGB–Bund tightener to capture the theme of convergence to the eurozone (Hungary: Euro vision, dated 4 June).
