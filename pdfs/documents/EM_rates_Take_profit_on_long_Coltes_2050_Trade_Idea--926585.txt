# EM rates: Take profit on long Coltes 2050

We take profit on our long Coltes 2050 position, booking a gain of 56bps (278k). We believe the long Coltes trade may have room for upside as the market discount fiscal consolidation ahead. However, we prefer to lock in profits as the curve could see consolidation after a meaningful downward trend and after the FOMC surprised markets on the hawkish side at its June meeting. We will monitor for new opportunities for expressing our bullish Coltes view. We entered on 1 June (after the first presidential election round) as we expected a sizeable premium compression in the curve due to expectations of fiscal improvement in the coming years (see EM rates: Entering a long 2050 Coltes position).
