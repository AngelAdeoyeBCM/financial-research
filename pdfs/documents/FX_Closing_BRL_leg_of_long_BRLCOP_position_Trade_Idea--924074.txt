# FX: Closing BRL leg of long BRLCOP position

As the BRL is reacting negatively to the latest headlines potentially impacting Flavio Bolsonaro’s candidacy to become president of Brazil, we are closing the short USDBRL leg of our long BRLCOP position. We book a loss of 0.4% (USD12k). We exit as increased momentum seems possible, potentially impacting Bolsonaro’s chances. We see the BRL as susceptible to a correction given that its technical position is overbought. We are keeping the long USDCOP leg as we expect the COP to continue correcting the sharp outperformance of the past months due to the proximity of Colombia’s presidential election (31 May). Given heightened uncertainty about the outcome and the bi-modal impact for the market, we expect the currency to underperform further, reflecting concern around the event. We entered the position on 4 May, expecting the BRL to continue performing well amid a positive terms of trade shock due to high oil prices and the currency’s high carry. On the COP side, our expectation was for underperformance as Election Day nears (see FX: Going long BRLCOP, dated 4 May).
