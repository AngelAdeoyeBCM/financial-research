# FX: Fade AUDUSD strength

Joshua Wilcock, G10 FX Strategist | Alex Jekov, Head of G10 FX Strategy, Europe | BNP Paribas London Branch. We came into the year long AUD as part of our consensus trades basket, which we closed today (FX: Closing consensus trades basket for a small gain), as historically consensus trades no longer perform in FX after the first week of the year. Instead, they tend to underperform in the coming two weeks, with hit ratios of 70% and 60% in weeks 2 and 3, respectively (see FX: Monetising year-ahead consensus, 11 December). The AUD is not only a consensus trade but also well positioned, with our positioning metrics showing AUD longs at a score of +43 (−/+ 50 scale; see Figure 1). BNP Paribas STEER suggests that AUDUSD is trading rich to other asset classes, in particular global equities, which the model views as the primary driver of the pair (see Figure 2). The model does not currently view front-end rate differentials as important, but we note that relative to front-end rate differentials, the move in AUDUSD looks less stretched. Stretched valuations and positioning aside, we are comfortable trading AUD from the short side.

- We see Australian superannuation funds as an ongoing source of AUD outflows (see Superannuation fund flows still weigh on AUD, dated 9 December 2025).
- One part of the bullish consensus year-ahead outlook on the AUD is the view that it stands to benefit from higher commodity prices, particularly in metals. That said, when we look at the weights and prices of Australia’s exports, we do not see a pickup in Australia’s terms of trade despite some metals prices rising recently.
- While we see scope for the RBA to deliver rate hikes this year, this may take time to play out (see AUD rates: Pay Feb/ May RBA spread, 5 December 2025), and given flow considerations, we see it as better expressed in rates than FX.

## KEY MESSAGES

We go short AUDUSD, as we find consensus trades tend to underperform after the first week of the year. As a consensus long, the AUD could be liable to a reversal lower, with positioning and valuations both stretched according to our metrics. Trade idea: Short USD10mn AUDUSD spot. Spot ref: 0.6702 (13:48 GMT). Target: 0.6500. Stop: 0.6805.
