# EM rates: Take profit on THB 5y5y payer

Sources: Bloomberg, Macrobond, BNP Paribas Fig. 1: THOR 5y5y has sold-off Chandresh Jain, EM Asia FX and Rates Strategist | MAR | Joey Li, Markets Analyst, APAC | BNP Paribas Singapore Branch. We take profit on our THOR u30-5y payer trade idea (see EM rates: Add THB 5y5y payer, dated 15 September), capturing 24bp (USD480k) on the trade. We initiated this trade after Thailand's new prime minister, Anutin Charnvirakul, formed a cabinet of experienced technocrats and signaled that reviving economic growth was the top priority. The interest rate curve had bull flattened significantly in the prior months, and we anticipated a potential sell off in the long-end. Our view has largely played out, with the government implementing measures to support the economy, including cash handouts and debt restructuring programs. They are also exploring ways to break the strong correlation between gold and the Thai baht, which has appreciated significantly and negatively impacted exports and tourism in 2025. Looking ahead, we think that Thai rates can rally a bit into the year-end due to sizable bond maturities in December. While we maintain our view that long-end rates can continue to sell off, we think it's prudent to take profit on the trade now and re-enter at better levels. We close our THOR u30-5y position at 2.05%, capturing a 24bp (USD480k) profit on the trade. We take profit on our THOR u30-5y payer trade idea (see EM rates: Add THB 5y5y payer, dated 15 September) as we have reached our target, capturing 29bp (USD580k) on the trade. We initiated this trade after Thailand's new prime minister, Anutin Charnvirakul, formed a cabinet of experienced technocrats and signaled reviving economic growth as his top priority. The interest rate curve had bull flattened significantly in the months prior to our trade entry, and we anticipated a potential sell-off in the long end. Our view has largely played out, with the government implementing measures, such as cash handouts and debt resolution programs to support the economy. Tourism has also picked up in Q4, which was supportive for the economy. Although the Bank of Thailand (BoT) cut its policy rate to 1.25% in December, this was in line with market expectations. Most recently, the BoT has continued to stress limited space for further easing, contributing to a further selloff in rates. Given the trade has reached our target, we close our THOR u30-5y position at 2.10%, capturing a 29bp (USD580k) profit on the trade. We will look to reassess and potentially reenter after the elections.
