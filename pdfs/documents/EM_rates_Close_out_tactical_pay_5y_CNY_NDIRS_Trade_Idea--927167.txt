# EM rates: Close out tactical pay 5y CNY NDIRS

. 29/06/2026 1

RKE. The trade idea we opened in EM rates: Tactical pay 5y CNY NDIRS on 29 May has reached its stop-loss. We close it at 1.458% for a loss of 5.95bp (-USD119k out of USD20k DV01 notional). The trade initially performed in line with our expectations, as the People’s Bank of China gradually drained excess liquidity. However, further upside momentum was missing because there was no catalyst for China rates to rise significantly. The PBoC also started to net inject liquidity a fter interbank rates (e.g., DR001 at 1.50%) had been squeezed modestly above the policy rate of 1.40%. We held the position, believing that downside risk was limited, given that the PBoC is unlikely to revert to the previous excess‐liquidity environment. We interpret the Lujiazui Forum announcements on 18 June – narrowing the interest-rate corridor, shifting the benchmark from DR007 to DR001, and expanding overnight reverse‐repo operations and related measures – as fine-tuning of liquidity management rather than a change in monetary-policy stance. Nevertheless, some market participants may have read these actions as further easing, especially in light of weak May economic and credit demand data, leading China rates to rally. We still think that the policy rate is likely to stay unchanged for the remainder of the year, although the PBoC’s policy remains appropriately loose (see China’s reflation: Enduring beyond energy shock, dated 29 June). Our outlook reflects ongoing NIM pressure, a lower growth target and modest reflation. Should Q2 growth miss expectations, we expect a fiscal response – potentially an accelerated pace of government-bond issuance – rather than further monetary easing. Overall, we anticipate China rates to remain range-bound, with limited downside risk. Ann Jiang, APAC Markets Strategist | MAR | BNP Paribas Hong Kong Branch
