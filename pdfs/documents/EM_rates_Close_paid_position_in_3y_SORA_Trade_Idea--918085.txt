# EM rates: Close paid position in 3y SORA

1: 3y SORA We close our paid 3y SORA rate trade idea, as it has traded through our stop-loss level. We initiated the idea earlier last month, anticipating a hawkish signal from the Monetary Authority of Singapore. We had expected the MAS to gradually reduce policy accommodativeness through both FX and rates. Our view was based on Singapore experiencing a reflationary phase with robust growth and bottoming-out inflation (see Singapore: Reflation set to change MAS and market views, dated 7 January). Initially, the SORA fixing moved higher, but its inherent volatility makes it challenging to determine whether this represents a sustained trend or a short-term fluctuation (Figure 2). As a result, we suspect that investors may be reducing their paid positions in SORA rates, likely due to the negative carry associated with holding these positions. The MAS did indeed send a hawkish signal at its January meeting, revising up its inflation forecasts and highlighting upside risks to growth and inflation. Although rates initially sold off in the days following the meeting, the recent global selloff in tech stocks, commodities, and cryptocurrencies has led to a broad-based rally across global rates (Figure 1). Although we continue to see a risk that the MAS reduces policy accommodativeness ahead, we respect our stop-loss level and close our H26, 3y SORA idea at 1.562% for a loss of 18.8bp (-USD282k).
