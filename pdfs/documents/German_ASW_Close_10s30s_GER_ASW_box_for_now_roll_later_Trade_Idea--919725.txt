# German ASW: Close 10s30s GER ASW box for now, roll later

. 02/03/2026 1

RKE. We close our 10s30s GER ASW box (invoice spread) instead of rolling the position over automatically, for a loss of 7.6bp or EUR152k on the current roll (cumulative profit of EUR70k since inception, see German ASWs: Box in with a 10s30s ASW box steepener, dated 21 March 2024). We prefer to close the position given the near-term risk that the curve flattens further on the back of a sustained rise in energy prices due to the conflict in the Middle East. We would seek to re-enter the position later at better levels. We still think the trade can structurally perform (30y Buxl ASW can richen vs the 10y Bund ASW) on the back of paying flows from the Dutch pension fund transition in the approach to 1 Jan uary 2027, and as we think the shape of the German curve still has room to normalise versus the swap curve. Close 10s30s GER ASW box at: −38bp for a loss of 7.6bp or EUR152k since inception. Entered at -30.4bp. Target -15bp. Stop: -53bp.
