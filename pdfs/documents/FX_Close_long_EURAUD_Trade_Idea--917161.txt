# FX: Close long EURAUD

We initiated a short AUD trade on 9 January (via short AUDUSD that we subsequently rotated into long EURAUD on 12 January). This was to capture our view that Australia superannuation funds would be an ongoing source of AUD selling flows (amid stable hedge ratios) and that positioning and valuations were stretched. Over the past week, EURAUD has traded to new lows. Our view on Australian superannuation funds has been proven wrong by a report this week from the second-largest Australian superannuation fund looking to raise FX hedge ratios on USD assets. This has likely contributed to recent AUD outperformance and leads us to move to the sidelines in AUD. Trade idea: Close long EURAUD at 1.71 stop-loss for a 1.88% loss (-USD188k; notional: 10mn).
