# FX: Exit long USDCOP position

We exit the long USDCOP position that was opened on 4 May, booking a loss of 0.6% (USD17k). We are exiting the trade because the position has breached our stop-loss level. The sharp rally in COP over the past several days has renewed its outperformance versus peers. Given the first-round presidential election on 31 May is still some time away, we expect the market has room to price in a higher risk premium and narrow this outperformance gap, given the proximity of the COP to the election. To recap, we entered the position in anticipation of the COP trailing as Election Day nears (see FX: Going long BRLCOP, dated 4 May).
