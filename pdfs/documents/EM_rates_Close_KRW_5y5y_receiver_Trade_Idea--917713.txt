# EM rates: Close KRW 5y5y receiver

Chandresh Jain, EM Asia FX and Rates Strategist | MAR | Joey Li, Markets Strategist, APAC | BNP Paribas Singapore Branch. Sources: Bloomberg, Macrobond, BNP Paribas Fig. 1: Long-end KRW rates have sold off, following the moves in JPY rates and USDKRW We close our received position in KRW H31,5y NDIRS at a loss of 19.5bp (-USD292.5k), as it has traded through our stop-loss level. We initiated the idea in January, following a hawkish Bank of Korea policy meeting, as we believed that the market's pricing of more than two rate hikes by end-2027 was excessive (see EM rates: Receive KRW 5y5y as it may be capped, dated 15 January). Our view remains that the current policy rate remains appropriate, and we see a high bar for the central bank to move rate in either direction during this period. We also expected WGBI inflows to support a rally in the long-end. KRW rates have sold off, given its correlation with the JGB curve and talks about a supplementary budget in Korea amid President Lee's recent comments. Although KRW rates experienced a relief rally after the JGBs calmed and USDKRW moved lower, the USD's subsequent strengthening amidst a broad-based risk-off environment and squaring of positions in consensus trades led to a USDKRW rebound and another sell-off in KRW rates (Figure 1). The upward move in oil prices also contributed to the sell-off in KRW rates from an inflation perspective. While our medium-term view on back-end KRW rates remains unchanged, recent market dynamics have highlighted the potential for other factors, such as global fiscal concerns and FX moves, to dominate price action in the near term. Therefore, we close our H31,5y receiver trade idea at 3.73% and will look to re-enter at more favorable levels. We close our receive KRW H31,5y NDIRS trade idea at a loss of xxbp (USDxxxk), as it has reached our stop-loss level. However, since our entry, KRW rates have failed to rally and instead sold off, initially driven by a sharp selloff in long-end JGBs amid concerns over Japanese election-related announcements (Figure 1). Although KRW rates experienced a relief rally after the JGBs calmed and USDKRW moved lower, the USD's subsequent strengthening amidst a broad-based risk-off environment and positioning squaring in consensus trades led to a USDKRW rebound and another selloff in KRW rates (Figure 1). While our medium-term view on back-end KRW rates remains unchanged, recent market dynamics have highlighted the potential for other factors, such as global fiscal concerns and FX movements, to dominate price action in the near term. Therefore, we are closing our H31,5y receiver position at 3.70% and will look to re-enter at more favorable levels, potentially with a wider stop-loss.
