# FX: Short NOKSEK

1: NOKSEK is rich vs BNP Paribas STEER fair value We go short NOKSEK around parity to fade the recent rally. NOKSEK has been a key beneficiary of the market positioning for the energy trade in FX (long energy exporters versus short energy importers) since the outbreak of conflict in the Middle East. However the cross has now rallied to around parity, a level it has not persistently traded above since 2023. It also trades expensive to its BNP Paribas STEER fair value of 0.9752 (Figure 1). Rich valuations and stretched long positioning in the cross (Figure 2) suggest market positioning for the energy trade is now well reflected in the cross, we think. At these levels we think there is an asymmetry to the downside where if oil prices go up, given positioning and valuations the cross may not rise much further whereas the cross could be particularly vulnerable if oil prices fall. The cross rallied after Norges Bank surprised the market by hiking its policy rate by 25bp to 4.25% at its May meeting. Norges Bank hawkishness is now well discounted in NOK in our view and the rally following the meeting presents an attractive entry point to go short NOKSEK.

## KEY MESSAGES

We add a NOKSEK short, targeting 0.96. Both positioning and valuations appear stretched and we expect the trade to perform well if oil prices fail to rally further. After today’s surprise Norges Bank rate hike we think the extent to which NOK is likely to be supported by Norges Bank rate hike pricing will be more limited.
