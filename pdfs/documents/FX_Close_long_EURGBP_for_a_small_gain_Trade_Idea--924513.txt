# FX: Close long EURGBP for a small gain

We close the long EURGBP spot trade that was initiated on 6 May, exiting the trade for a small gain (0.37%; USD 37k). Going into local elections in the UK on 7 May, we viewed valuations and positioning as consistent with limited UK political risk premium in the cross. This created an asymmetry to the topside, we thought, given the risks of the Labour party’s leadership changing and the potential for a GBP-negative shift in policy. The election outcome made this change more likely, with the focus having turned to the pathway for Andy Burnham, a former Labour MP and now mayor of Greater Manchester, to replace Keir Starmer as prime minister. That said, Burnham on Monday ruled out any changes to the existing fiscal rules as well as an exemption for defence spending that he had previously put forward. At face value, this reduced possible GBP-negative tail risks surrounding potential future changes in policy, in turn going against our long EURGBP trade idea. With the earliest possible date for the Makerfield by-election the 18 June, and momentum going against the trade, we deem it prudent to close the trade for a small gain. This leaves us with short GBP delta in our strategy portfolio via our fiscal long/ short basket (see FX: Extend target to 8% and trail stop on fiscal basket, 23 March) and our SX5E higher/ EURGBP higher dual digital trade idea (see Hybrid insights: SX5E and EURGBP higher, dated 29 January).
