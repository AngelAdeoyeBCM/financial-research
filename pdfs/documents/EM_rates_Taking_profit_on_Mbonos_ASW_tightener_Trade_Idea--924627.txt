# EM rates: Taking profit on Mbonos ASW tightener

We take profit on our 2038 Mbonos-10y TIIE-F asset swap receiver position, as the spread has moved in our favour, booking a gain of 11.2bp (USD168k). The asset-swap spread narrowed given the recent broader rates sell-off, after Banxico concluded its easing cycle at the latest policy meeting, paving the way for Mbonos to outperform as the peso strengthened. Yet, with local rates now hovering near their post-conflict peak and the possibility of heightened volatility—a reversal that would boost swaps more than bonds and widen the spread—we decided to book profits and look for a more favorable entry point. To recap, we initiated the position on 27 April, arguing that external uncertainty was creating an asymmetric risk that favoured a tightening of the wide spread. The trade is also supported by the prospect of increased foreign ownership of Mbonos, underpinned by Mexico’s stable political environment, a robust MXN and modest fiscal pressures (see EM rates: Entering 2038 Mbonos ASW receiver).
