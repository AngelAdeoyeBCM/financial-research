# FX: Close out short USDKRW

. 12/01/2026 1

RKE. We close our short USDKRW (26May25 NDF) idea for flat P&L, with the pair having reached our trailed-to-entry stop level. On 21 November 2025, we initiated a short USDKRW 6m NDF trade idea, with a view that the flow picture would improve over the medium term, allowing the KRW to better reflect its underlying fundamentals – see FX: Fading KRW weakness, sell USDKRW 6m NDF for the full rationale. Since then, local authorities’ growing concern over the imbalanced flow picture and currency weakness has resulted in a number of measures being announced to encourage repatriation and investment in local assets. Reports also suggest the NPS has started strategic hedging and that the BoK intervened in the FX market to defend the won. These developments prompted a sharp decline in USDKRW from 1480 to 1435. After this performance, coming into the new year, we viewed it prudent to trail the stop on our trade to our entry level (1460.50) – see FX: Trail stop on short USDKRW (dated 7 January). The pair has seen a sharp retracement over the last few sessions, and we have now reached our trailed-stop level. We think this move reflects local retail investors’ vociferous appetite for foreign assets, and possible position washout of KRW longs. Indeed, BNP Paribas Global FX Positioning Analysis suggests FX investors have reduced KRW longs from a score of +15 to +6 (on a +/ -50 scale) since late December. We respect our stop and close our trade idea for a flat P&L. However, we continue to believe the flow picture will improve over the coming months, as the announced measures get implemented, and later due to WGBI inflows and the risk of the NPS pivoting its asset allocation in favour of domestic assets. The pace of this USDKRW rise might also make authorities uncomfortable, and raise the risk of further intervention to stabilise the currency. In turn, we will be keen to re-engage in KRW longs at more stretched valuations.
