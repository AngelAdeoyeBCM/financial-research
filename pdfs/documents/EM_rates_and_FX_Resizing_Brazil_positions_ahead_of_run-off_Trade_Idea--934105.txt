# EM rates and FX: Resizing Brazil positions ahead of run-off

As we head into the presidential election run-off on Sunday 25 October, we are scaling down the size of our current long positions. Specifically, we are reducing our Jan29 DI receiver exposure to USD5k DV01 from usd10k DV01 and our short EURBRL position to USD5mn from USD15mn (see details below). We keep long exposure in both FX and rates, as the odds remain skewed towards an opposition victory on Sunday. Under such an outcome we still see room for both assets to outperform over the next couple of months. Nevertheless, as the market reaction to an unexpected outcome would be outsized, we prefer to reduce risk to better manage the P/L impact if such a contingency materializes. We entered both positions after the first round on 4 October as we saw room for a rally in both assets, in light of the better-than-expected performance of candidate Flavio Bolsonaro (see EM FX and rates: Long BRL and receive DI on bullish first round election results, dated 5 October).
