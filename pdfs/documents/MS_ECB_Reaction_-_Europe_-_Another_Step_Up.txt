# ECB Reaction - Europe - Another Step Up

As expected, the ECB increased rates by 25bp today. The messaging remained open. Projections see higher inflation over the whole forecast horizon, with 2028 slightly above target. The statement | Rates up to 2.5%: As widely expected, the ECB raised the policy rates today by 25bp to 2.5%. The rate hike was motivated by an expectation that inflation is set to remain above target for an extended period and to underscore the commitment to ensure that inflation returns to target. Looking ahead, volatility of energy prices and the uncertainty of the inflation outlook suggest that all options remain on the table. The assessment that the ECB remains well positioned to address the shock remained in the policy paragraph, providing some perspective on the appropriate speed of tightening and supporting a gradual pace. The remark that the ECB is "closely monitoring" the situation, introduced in April, was dropped. While we don't think this has any deeper significance for the likelihood of additional hikes, it could be a signal that the Council sees no immediate urgency and sees itself in a relatively comfortable position to await additional data. Future monetary policy decisions remain data dependent and will be taken meeting-by-meeting. Projections | 2028 slightly above target: As we expected the updated projections in September do not differ much from June. We flag two interesting points. First, growth in 2026 (0.9%Y from 0.8%Y) and 2027 (1.4%Y from 1.2%Y) was revised up, a consequence of the latest data prints and more confidence in the "structural" strength in the economy. Second, the path of inflation is taken higher in 2027 but also in 2028. 2028 HICP now stands slightly above target (2.1%Y). This does not only reflect an uptick of energy CPI as forecasters also lifted the path of core by 10bp both in 2027 and 2028. We are eager to see whether the inflation path reflects a later cut-off than we assumed (17 August) and/or a re-assessment of the expected transmission from energy to core. With the recent moves in energy markets, significant focus will also be on the alternative scenarios, which will be released with the details of the projections at 3.45pm CET. The press conference focus: We expect most of the press conference to focus on the ECB's next steps, and the recent developments in energy prices (primarily natural gas) and their impact on core inflation path. We also expect questions on the new set of projections, particularly the upward revision of core inflation upwards. Another focus is likely the evolution of the risk assessment and the strength of the euro area economy in the face of a renewed energy price shock. Recent financial market developments, in particular increasing long-term bond yields could be another focus. We also expect questions on ECB leadership changes.
