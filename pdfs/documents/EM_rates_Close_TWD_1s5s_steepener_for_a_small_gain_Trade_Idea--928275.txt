# EM rates: Close TWD 1s5s steepener for a small gain

. 16/07/2026 1

RKE. We close the TWD 12-Jun-26, 1s5s steepener at 48bp for a profit of 5.5bp (USD55k out of USD10k DV01), see Taiwan: CBC to hike rates, but unlikely to do so in June, dated 12 June. Taiwan rates traded in a bull-flattening manner after the June meeting of the central bank (CBC) and a correction in oil prices following the de-escalation in the Middle East. That said, the curve has shown resilience – the 5y looks supported at 2.10%, while the 1s5s bottomed at 30–35bp – which we attribute to the strong growth backdrop, the wealth effect and market concerns over Taiwan’s inflation. Since the end of June, the curve has steepened due to June inflation being higher than widely expected and the rise in oil prices. We believe it is prudent to take profit on our 1s5s steepener now and wait to re-engage at a more attractive level. Although the outlook for Taiwanese equities and the tech industry remains resilient, market expectations for the Fed have turned less hawkish following the recent US inflation print. Additionally, we think regional beta is less supportive, as there’s scope for KRW rates to rally alongside easing concerns over FX weakness (EM rates: Closing paid KRW 1s3s10s fly; add tactical receiver, dated 16 July 2026). Our base case remains that the CBC will start its hiking cycle in December this year, followed by another hike in Q1 2027. Taiwan’s June inflation poses upside risks to our 2026 annual CPI forecast of 2.0% for headline as well as core inflation. We also believe these readings are stronger than what the CBC envisaged in its inflation projection of 1.9% for 2026. While lower oil prices may mitigate some concerns, we think that broad-based wage increases, a tight labour market and strong economic growth will keep Taiwan rates steep in the medium term.
