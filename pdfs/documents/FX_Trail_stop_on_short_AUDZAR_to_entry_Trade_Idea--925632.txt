# FX: Trail stop on short AUDZAR to entry

We initiated a short AUDZAR trade idea on 7 May to reflect our view that positioning and valuations had become stretched after the cross benefited from higher energy prices. Our rationale was that fresh gains would be capped, in the event of rising energy prices, but that declines in the cross may be considerable if prices fell. Since then, energy prices have receded and the cross has indeed fallen. We still think this potential asymmetry is in play, and continue to hold the trade. Our metrics suggest positioning is still long (see Figure 1), but we deem it prudent to trail the stop to the entry level of 11.8342. We continue to target a move sub- 11.50, to 11.30.
