# FX vol: Sell USDCNH risk reversal through long call fly

. 24/02/2026 1

B N P PA GL OB ET S A PP

## KEY MESSAGES

- USDCNH risk reversals screen as rich compared with realised spot-vol.
- Post-Lunar-New-Year seasonality indicates bullish spot risks, and the USDCNH spot is cheap according to the MarFATM macro model.
- Trade idea: Buy 6m 6.85–6.95–7.05 call fly in USD 40mn x USD 80mn x USD 40mn (18.7bp premium; 6.8750 spot ref). Elevated risk reversal: The six-month USDCNH risk reversal has risen to a nine-month high at 0.3pp bid for calls, up more than 0.2pp since early-February. However, as we highlighted in FX vol strategy week ly, realised spot-vol covariance has not increased in tandem, leaving risk reversals screening as rich at current levels (Figure 1). This makes a short-vanna (shorter vol on higher spot) structure attractive, in our view. Seasonals and valuation signal bullish risks to USDCNH: Our Lunar-New-Year seasonality analysis flags positive moves in USDCNH the week after the new year (Figure 2). Meanwhile, spot screens as cheap in the MarFATM macro framework (Figure 3), suggesting a long-delta bias may be favourable. With low implied vol, USDCNH spot also screens as a high carry/vol currency pair. While this structure has a very small directional delta exposure, we think adding a positive carry bias – supported by the positive delta signals elsewhere – helps the short-vol nature of this trade idea. Short vol in a limited-loss format: The characteristics of USDCNH spot and vol behaviour are similar to pegged currencies: very low realised vol with day-to-day moves strongly influenced by fixings. For a fully pegged currency, vol pricing may be discontinuous: at-the-money vol may trend lower as stable day-to-day FX levels mean realised vol is extremely low. However, a change in the peg regime may be under-priced due to this extremely low anchor. In the case of US DCNH, we think this suggests that selling call vol in a limited-loss format is prudent. We add a call fly with strikes set such that current USDCNH spot is in-the-money. This structure has a positive decay profile, and while it loses positive delta if spot rises above 7.00 in the short-term, the maximum loss is equal to the premium spent. All else equal, the structure earns elevated risk premium from selling topside vol with the high strikes decay more quickly than the near-the-money option, earning premium over the life of the structure. In the best case where USDCNH spot is 6.95 in six months, this structure would be worth seven-times its initial premium.
