# EM rates: Taking profit on Mbonos ASW long position

We take profit on our 2034 Mbonos-10y TIIE-F asset swap receiver position after the spread moved in our favour, booking a gain of 6.4bp (USD96k). The asset-swap spread tightened as the bond curve outperformed the swap curve while broader rates rallied. We still believe that, with Banxico having completed its easing cycle, Mbonos have the potential to outperform the swap curve, although a weakening MXN could decrease that outperformance marginally. We chose to lock in profits now, ahead of the Banxico meeting and the potential upcoming bond-switch operation. A less hawkish-than-expected Banxico tone or a bond-switch program that sells long-end bonds could weigh on the asset-swap spread. We will wait for a more attractive entry point. To recap, we entered the trade on 1 June, noting that spread tightening remained the main risk amid US-Iran talks. We also expect Mbonos to bounce back after their recent lag, following the usual pattern after the government’s bond-swap operation. The 4 June coupon payment should bolster the Mbono curve as investors redeploy the proceeds (see EM rates: Entering long 2034 Mbonos ASW trade).
