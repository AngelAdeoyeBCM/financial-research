# FX: Squaring USD risk on Fed independence concerns

Alex Jekov, Head of G10 FX Strategy, Europe | Sam Lynton-Brown CFA, Global Head of Macro Strategy | Oliver Brennan, FX Volatility Strategist | Joshua Wilcock, London Branch | Calvin Tse, Head of Americas Macro Strategy and US Economics | Ishan Gurnani, Securities Corp. Sources: BNP Paribas, Bloomberg, Macrobond Fig.1: Positioning is short USD On Friday, we added an AUDUSD short, tilting our portfolio tactically long USDs. Newsflow over the weekend around the United States Department of Justice serving the Fed with grand-jury subpoenas challenges our tactical long USD view. We see two key drivers for the USD currently: the US’s economic outperformance, and the Fed independence concerns. This weekend’s newsflow will likely create a greater focus on Fed independence concerns for the market, fattening the left tail for the USD. This pushes back against our tactical bullish USD call from last Friday, which was based on

1. short USD FX positioning (Fig.1),
2. seasonality of consensus unwinds (see FX: Closing Consensus trades basket for a small gain),
3. US economic strength (see US December jobs: Glass-half full for labor market, fuller for growth). Similar to Q2 last year, EURUSD higher will prove the best expression of US institutional credibility concerns, in our view. As such, we deem it prudent to adjust our trades. We close our USDCAD call for a small profit and roll our AUDUSD short into a long EURAUD trade. This achieves two things: first, it flips our overall USD exposure to be net neutral, and second, it allows us to participate in further Fed credibility concerns via EURAUD, as we expect the EUR to be more sensitive than the AUD to Fed credibility being eroded. In addition to this, we hold a 4m EURUSD higher, SPX lower dual digital (see Safe haven shift? EURUSD higher in US equity selloffs, dated 21 November 2025) and a fiscal basket (see FX: Hedging for fiscal vulnerabilities, dated 5 November 2025), both of which should benefit if the market transitions into a Q2 2025 ‘Sell America’ dynamic. Should we see more days like Monday, where equities are down and EURUSD is higher, we see room for hedging of USD risk by foreign investors to return, but we believe investors will need to see more evidence that this dynamic is going to persist (see What drives FX hedge ratios?, 3 July 2025).

## KEY MESSAGES

Fed independence concerns fatten the left tail for the USD. With these risks back in focus, we deem it prudent to adjust our trades to be net neutral USDs overall. EURAUD should benefit from Fed credibility concerns as we expect EUR to be more sensitive than AUD to the theme. Trade ideas:

- Close long 3m USDCAD 1.40 call (USD +7k). Spot ref: 1.3880 (10:10 GMT).
- Convert short AUDUSD into long EURAUD spot: buy USD10mn EURUSD spot. Spot ref: 1.1682 (10:10 GMT).
- Long EURAUD. Spot ref: 1.7418 (10:10 GMT). Target: 1.80. Stop: 1.71.
