# FX: Adding to long USDJPY exposure on the dip

We think risk-reward is attractive to buy the dip in USDJPY and therefore enter a call with an EKO. The case for USDJPY to appreciate remains strong, in our view, with the B ank of Japan still well behind the curve, JPY positioning neutral, on our metrics, and additional fiscal being deployed in Japan (see Japan: Persistent reflationism in an inflationary era, dated 23 July). To the extent that today’s USDJPY move reflects Ministry of Finance FX intervention which notably has not been confirmed, we argue that without a change in monetary policy in Japan, USDJPY is unlikely to sustain losses for long, similar to what happened during April’s intervention. Additionally, we do not expect recent news flow concerning Japan’s Government Pension Investment Fund to result in material FX strength (see Global Macro: The Great Reversal? Japan’s potential domestic pivot, dated 27 July). We see USDJPY continuing to grind higher and prefer to position for topside risks in USDJPY spot whilst staying short gamma. Front-end USDJPY risk reversals have moved further in favour of puts and screen rich (too bid-for-puts) compared to spot-vol correlation. We think this indicates market concerns over potential FX intervention and GPIF asset reallocation, which could limit near-term topside in the pair. We think markets are already expecting a hawkish hold at the July BoJ meeting, and for the JPY to benefit from a hawkish message the central bank would need not only to discuss increasing the pace of rate hikes but more explicitly hint at the September meeting as being in play for a rate hike, which we deem unlikely. In addition to our long USDJPY spot trade idea entered on 23 June at 161.36 level (see Adding long USD carry via long USDJPY and USDCHF), we now add a 3m (30 Oct) USDJPY 160 call with European KO at 163.50 in USD30mn for 24bp (spot ref: 159.42).
