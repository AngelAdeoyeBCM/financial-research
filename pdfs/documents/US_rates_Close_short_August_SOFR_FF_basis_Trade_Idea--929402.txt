# US rates: Close short August SOFR/FF basis

Back in May, we entered into a long June versus short August SOFR/FF basis spread trade to position for upward pressure on funding rates through the second half of this year. Our reasoning was based on the seasonality and guidance for elevated Treasury issuance and above-target TGA balances, particularly for the months of July/August and October/November (see US rates: How do you like them ample reserves, dated 18 May). Most of the market started to position this way early on. The Treasury has issued $300bn in bills for July and TGA balances are back to target levels, but repo has still traded at somewhat soft levels – at or slightly below IORB. Most of the tailwinds from repo ha ve come from TGA volatility (particularly on month-end drops due to HHS-related payments), stable dealer balance sheets, and accelerated growth in money market fund assets accompanied by a decline in the weighted average maturity of their holdings. We exit our position for a loss of 2bp but continue to expect marginal tightening in repo spreads into the fall. We maintain our view that SOFR/FF should settle within an equilibrium range of −2bp and −4bp until debt ceiling dynamics return in late 2027. Trade: Close long SERFFM6 (June) vs short SERFFQ6 (August). Entry: −3bp. Close: −1bp. Profit/(Loss): −2bp. Size: $50k/01.
