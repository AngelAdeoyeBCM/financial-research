# EM rates: Taking profit on CLP basis receiver

We are taking profit on our CLP basis receiver trade, which has moved in our favor on the back of weakness in the currency, locking in a profit of 8bp (USD40k). Following developments in the Middle East and the ensuing broader EM FX sell-off, the CLP basis has moved sharply to the downside. We still expect further downside pressure on the CLP, as Chilean pension funds (AFPs) are likely to increase FX hedging to narrow the gap with their foreign assets. However, we are exiting the position now to book a profit before any potential reversal of USDCLP price action. We will continue to monitor the market for a suitable opportunity to re-enter the exposure. To recap, we entered the trade on 19 February anticipating increased FX hedging by AFPs, which is putting downward pressure on the CLP basis (see EM rates: Entering a CLP basis receiver).
