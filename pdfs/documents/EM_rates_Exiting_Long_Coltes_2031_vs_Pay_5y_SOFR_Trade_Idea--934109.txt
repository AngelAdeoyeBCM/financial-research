# EM rates: Exiting Long Coltes 2031 vs Pay 5y SOFR

As our long Coltes position has reached its stop-loss level, we are exiting at 2.575% (entry: 2.12%) and booking a loss of 44.8bp (448k). The position did not perform because of continuous supply pressure through auctions and debt-liability operations, with linkers showing particular underperformance after Colombia’s September CPI print failed to surprise to the upside, leading the market to believe that any inflation pressure coming from the El Niño weather event will take time to materialize. The recent UST rally also weighed on the position. We entered the position as we wanted to keep exposure to a potential positive surprise of financing by multilaterals (including IMF). We positioned through linkers in anticipation of unexpectedly high inflation due to El Niño (see EM rates: Long Coltes UVR 2031 vs Pay 5y SOFR, 1 October).
