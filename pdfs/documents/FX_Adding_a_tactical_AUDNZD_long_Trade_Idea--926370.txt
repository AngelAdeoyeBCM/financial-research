# FX: Adding a tactical AUDNZD long

. 15/06/2026 1

BNP P ARIBAS GLOBAL M ARKE TS AP P.

## KEY MESSAGES

- We think event risks in Australia (RBA decision) and New Zealand (Q1 GDP data) will be supportive of AUDNZD.
- AUDNZD trades cheap to fair value and positioning is light.
- Trade idea: Long AUDNZD Entry 1.2140; target 1.2350; stop-loss 1.1980; total allocation USD5mn We buy the AUD dip after the recent washout in market long positioning by adding a tactical AUDNZD long ahead of event risks in both Australia and New Zealand this week. We see room for front-end rate differentials to move in favor of the cross again. In Australia, we see a risk of a hawkish hold from the RBA. We agree with market pricing and consensus expectations for no change in interest rates this week, but there is scope for future hikes to be adde d (see AUD rates: Pay June/Aug RBA, dated 21 May). Optimism on the Middle East conflict and softer growth / employment data have reduced market conviction in an additional hike, but recent speeches have remained focused on risks to inflation. A continuation of this could deliver a hawkish surprise for markets. In New Zealand, we see downside risks for front-end rates and the NZD ahead of the Q1 GDP release. Consensus estimates are for 0.8% q/q, a touch below the RBNZ forecast of 1.0%. A weak number would indicate that growth was on a softer-than-expected footing heading into the conflict. In contrast, we think markets may look past strong data as much has shifted since Q1 for the global macroeconomy. Given the market’s aggressive pricing of RBNZ rate hikes, the larger reaction is likely if the data comes in soft, as there is ample space for those anticipated hikes priced to be dialed back. The above local catalysts suggest risks for front-end rates in opposite directions. This should put upward pressure on the AUDNZD cross, given it s good correlation with 2y rate spreads. Zooming out, while optimism on the conflict may weigh on the cross as the terms of trade gap narrows, we would note that the cross has remained supported despite recent weakness in commodity prices. We would also highlight that the RBNZ’s hawkish turn reflected conflict-driven upside risks to inflation while the RBA had become hawkish beforehand, making the New Zealand front end more sensitive to a fall in oil prices. This could limit any NZD recovery and should thus provide some insulation to our trade. We have been wary of positioning. Markets are long AUD and short NZD, according to our metrics (+17 and -11 respectively, net: +28; +/-50 scale). However, AUD longs appear to be more structural here given the shifting behavior of local superannuation funds (see FX: Superannuation fund flows – from bearish to bullish AUD, dated 27 January). As a result, while positioning is long, it is at the lower end of the recent ranges and so the risks of a large unwind may be relatively less here. Valuations are supportive, with our BNP Paribas MarFATM framework suggesting that AUD NZD is cheap, with the fair value of the cross closer to 1.2260. We thus think the quantitative case to go long here is also more compelling.
