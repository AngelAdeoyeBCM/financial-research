# EM rates: Closing flattener leg of our HUF trade

We close the flattener leg of our HUF IRS trade, U26 2y vs 22-Jan-27 3m FRA at 8bp for –29.5bp loss (USD295k), as it has hit our revised stop-loss level (see EM rates: Rolling M26 to U26 for HUF receivers, dated 17 June). As the NBH have communicated that they will deliver two additional 25bp cuts over the summer, the very front-end FRAs have remained quite well-anchored, whilst the 2y point has underperformed in this latest move higher in energy prices. We initiated this trade in EM rates: The case for HUF receivers, dated 20 April, and remain received the 5y point, targeting a move below 4.50% eventually. In addition to our 5y receiver, we maintain our 10y HGB–Bund tightener to capture the convergence to the eurozone theme (see Hungary: Euro vision, dated 4 June).
