# EM rates: Exiting 2y IBR–TIIE-F payer

We exit the 2y IBR–TIIE-F payer trade that was opened on 6 May (see EM rates: Entering 2y IBR–TIIE-F spread payer), booking a loss of 38bp (USD191k). We are exiting the position as April inflation data in Colombia failed to act as a catalyst for higher rates. Furthermore, news on the Council of State striking down the pension funds transfer decree was sparking a leg down in the IBR curve. Even though the proximity of Colombia’s first-round presidential election on 31 May should drive further premium in the IBR curve, we prefer to exit now. To recap, we entered the position following the recent and surprising monetary policy pause in Colombia. The IBR curve should retain its premium, signaling either a higher terminal rate or stronger inflation ahead. With the presidential election less than three weeks away, the premium seems further warranted given the potential for ongoing political pressure on the central bank under a new administration.
