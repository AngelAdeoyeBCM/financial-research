# DM rates: Trail stop on Long 1y EU HICPxT vs Q2 2026 Brent hedge

. 16/01/2026 1

RKE. The 1y EU HICPxT has reached 182.5bp, just 3.5bp short of our target. We initiated the trade idea at 167bp on 12 January (see Inflation: Long 1y EU HICPxT vs Q2 2026 Brent hedge, dated 12 January). While easing crude prices on news that US intervention may not be imminent affected the trade negatively, the upswing in natural gas prices, driven by cold weather expectations and lower-than-expected gas storge levels in Europe, has pushed the 1y higher to its current level. The situation in Iran remains highly uncertain, with a US strike on Iran expected by some. That will continue to create upside risk to Brent hence we are not considering the Brent hedge yet. Further, as we deem it likely that momentum in the gas market may linger for a bit before correcting, we extend our target by 10bp to 196bp. Additionally, we add a stop-win at 173bp to lock in EUR240k of profit.
