# EM rates: Take profit on long 3y MGS

Chandresh Jain, EM Asia FX and Rates Strategist | MAR | Joey Li, APAC Markets Analyst | BNP Paribas Singapore Branch. appreciating against USD We take profit on our long with FX implied funding, capturing a total profit of USD374.8k on the trade, including carry. We initiated the trade ahead of Bank Negara Malaysia’s MPC meeting in July 2025, expecting MYR rates to rally (see EM rates: Long 3y MGS ahead of July MPC meeting, dated 24 June 2025). We preferred going long bonds over receiving swaps, as implied yields were trading at lows amid the weak dollar environment back then, providing a positive carry to fund the position. On the other hand, receiving swaps was a negative carry trade. The trade has broadly performed since then – BNM delivered a rate cut in July as expected, while implied yields have moved higher from 2025 lows (Figure 1). Additionally, the rally in MGS has led us to be under-hedged in FX, which also benefited the trade, as the MYR has been appreciating against the USD over the same period (Figure 2). Looking ahead, we believe investors will expect BNM to remain on hold, as policy seems to be in a good place. While the trade remains slightly positive carry, we believe it is prudent to take profit on the trade now, given the recent the risk of selloff in MYR rates as economic growth momentum could steepen the curve. We close our long MGS position at 3.07% and unwind our FX implied legs for a total profit of USD374.8k (18.7bp), MGS rallied, while implied yields moved higher We extend our target on our two long bond (FX implied funding) trades, both of which we entered last June, when implied yields were trading at a low amidst the weak dollar environment. Long 2y KTB trade: We initiated our long position in 2y Korea Treasury Bonds (KTB), as it provided one of the best carry when funded with 12m NDF FX implied yields while having a very good credit rating (see EM rates: Long 2y KTB with FX implied funding, dated 4 June 2025). Since then, KRW rates have broadly sold off, as markets’ expectation from the Bank of Korea (BoK) shifted from rate cuts to rate hikes. Although the bond yield declined by 30-40bp, implied yields have risen even more sharply, increasing by 70- 80bp, thus keeping the trade positive carry. Furthermore, the significant decline in bond value resulted in us becoming over-hedged in FX, which in turn benefited our long USDKRW hedge in the forwards as the KRW depreciated over our holding period. Looking forward, we continue to like this trade. Front-end KRW rates have already sold off to extreme levels, in our view, with the IRS curve currently pricing 2-3 rate hikes by end-2027. We see the BoK on an extended hold, with a high bar for either rate cuts or rate hikes (see Bank of Korea: Shift to neutral stance supports prolonged rate hold, dated 15 January 2026).
