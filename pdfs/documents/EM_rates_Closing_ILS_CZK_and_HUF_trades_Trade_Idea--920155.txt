# EM rates: Closing ILS, CZK and HUF trades

Lior Kohanan, Global Macro Strategist | MAR | Burak Baskurt CFA, Global Head of EM Strategy | MAR. With the continued rise in energy prices, we close some of the more risk-on trade ideas in our EM portfolio. We take profit on our ILS front-end flattener at -19bp for a gain of 8bp (USD83k), which we initiated in EM rates: Re-entering ILS front-end flattener, on 17 November 2025. We also close our CZK–USD 1y1y tightener at 162bp for a loss of 89bp (USD891k) (initiated last week in EM rates: Receive CZK vs USD 1y1y), as we have traded through our stop. Finally, we close our HUF 1s3s flattener (entered on 2 December 2025) at 5bp, for a loss of 8bp (USD80k). Trade idea: Close ILS M26 6m vs M26 2y at -19bp (GMT 08:08). Trade idea: Close CZK 1y1y (vs 3m) vs SOFR 1y1y at 162bp (GMT 08:21). Trade idea: Close HUF M26 1s3s at 5bp (GMT 08:19).
