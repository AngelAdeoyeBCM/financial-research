# FX: Closing out long AUDNZD

. 24/06/2026 1

BNP P ARIBAS GLOBAL M ARKE TS AP P. We close the trade idea we initiated in FX: Adding a tactical AUDNZD long on 15 June for a profit of 0.72% (USD36k, spot ref: 1.22 22). The initial rationale for the trade was to position ahead of the Reserve Bank of Australia meeting and Q1 GDP report in New Zealand. The two events posed risks for front-end rates in opposite directions, which would have put upward pressure on the cross. The outlook at the RBA meeting was more balanced and Q1 GDP in New Zealand came in stronger than anticipated. We find it encouraging that despite neither catalyst materialising in a direction that would be supportive, the cross still trended higher, highlighting that our position was the correct one to capture the event asymmetry. We held the position through Australia’s May CPI report for a similar rationale but now opt to move to the sidelines ahead of the country’s May employment report. This event poses more two-sided risks for spot than others. A stronger report could see front-end meeting pricing shift to reflect a greater probability of an additional hike – we remain paid August RBA. However, if the data were to surprise to the downside (for the second consecutive time), then there is scope for AUD longs to further unwind. We thus think it makes sense to lock in our profits now. Ishan Gurnani, G10 FX Strategist | BNP Paribas Securities Corp.
