# FX: Trailing the stop on short EURAUD to entry

We initiated a short EURAUD trade idea on 2 October to add downside EUR protection amid OAT-spread widening. Although the OAT-Bund spread has not re-widened since initiating the trade, EUR crosses including EURAUD have remained heavy. From a positioning perspective our tracker suggests EUR shorts are historically stretched (see BNPP Global FX Positioning Tracker, dated 12 October), and EUR put convexity has built to a five-year high. However, our options strike map does not suggest that EURUSD strikes sub-1.10 are well owned (see FX vol strategy weekly: EUR downside dichotomy, dated 12 October) so we do not yet have confidence that France risks are adequately hedged in EUR. We continue to favour pairing EUR short exposure with longs in AUD as we are tactically constructive on the AUD outlook while global equities stay supported, rate differentials are wide (leading to attractive carry on the crosses), and Australia benefits from reduced fiscal vulnerabilities compared to elsewhere. We have increased our exposure to this theme through a EURUSD lower / AUDUSD higher dual digital (see FX vol roadmap: Fiscal spillovers, dated 13 October). We continue to hold the spot trade idea, targeting a move to 1.56, but deem it prudent to trail the stop to entry (1.61470), 1.19% from current spot (spot ref: 1.59678; 11:47 BST).
