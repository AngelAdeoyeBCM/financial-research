# EM rates: Take profit on the MXN basis payer

We are taking profit on our MXN basis payer trade as the basis has moved in our favor, booking a gain of 7.3bp (USD73k). The front-end basis recovered, moving higher after hitting lows amid the broader risk-off mood sparked by the Middle-East conflict. Although we still see multi-week upside potential, we are exiting the position now to lock in gains, given the likely negative month-end seasonality and the persisting Middle-East uncertainty. We will keep watching the market for an appropriate re-entry opportunity. To recap, we entered the trade on 10 March anticipating that potential larger seasonal remittance inflows (boosting USD liquidity) together with the March-April tax season (squeezing MXN liquidity) could push the basis higher (see EM rates: Entering an MXN basis payer).
