Why keys only build (131)

  1. Policy-rate indicators missing for US, JP, NZ, NO, SE, CA, AU. Only ind:{ea,gb,ch,cn}:policy_rate exist; the US has only rate:fed_funds.
  2. Event occurrences such as event:fomc@2026-09. The registry holds only series.
  3. Participant groups: real money, speculative, households, life insurers, banks, corporates.
  4. Built instruments: UST 2y/30y outrights and curves, OAT-Bund 10y, SOFR-TONA spread and basis.
  5. Unregistered crosses: fx:chfjpy, fx:audcad, fx:cadchf.
  6. No FX-intervention tool, so org:jp_mof stands in.
  7. Indicators built from measure × geo: ind:gb:gdp, ind:nz:*, ind:jp:term_premium.

Why statements can't resolve (54)

Design points

  • Keys merge. Beam S003, S033 and S080 all become fx:audcad | short | maintain.
  • Disagreement is countable. BofA's +75bp and the Fed dots' +50bp share ind:us:policy_rate | path | · | 2026.
  • "Constructive" on an asset-swap spread has no direction (FOMC S073).
  • 6m3m forward vol can't be built: Outright rejects a forward start on options.
  • Basis widening has no sign convention in DOC.md §5.
  • Revisions need vs prior_sep to differ from levels.
How to read a key

Position

Instrument, direction, action. Pairs use fx: in market convention, so "long JPY vs CHF" becomes fx:chfjpy | short. Actions: open, maintain, close, review.

State

Subject, variable, value, horizon. Qualifiers sit in brackets (flows[real_money]). priced is kept apart from path so "underpriced" reads as path vs priced.

Causal

State → state. A semicolon joins states on one side. Subjects starting with ? (hatched) don't exist and can't be built.
